## Lesson 68 Q10 change
- V2: 68-V2-Q10 tested person-clitic placement (=m) in Haig's full past-negative transitive sentence «min seg-eke=m ne-kušt».
- V3: 68-V3-Q10 tests the source-attested order ne- before de- in Ahmadi's Sorani past-progressive intransitive example «heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe».
- Original item 68-Q07 («نەدەنووسی») remains HOLD as an exact Kurdish-script form. V3 does not claim the Ahmadi example is an exact equivalent of «نەدەنووسی».
- This is a source-backed replacement objective for the general lesson topic of past-progressive negation; it teaches the segmentation shown in the cited example, not a full person paradigm or a universal rule for every verb class.

## Source basis
Sina Ahmadi (2021), *A Formal Description of Sorani Kurdish Morphology*, Rule 7.9, lines 2493–2532: ne- is the negative prefix, de-/e- is the modal prefix in the intransitive past-progressive rule, and the source example is «heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe» (translated by the author as “(we) were not fleeing away”):
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354494225_A_Formal_Description_of_Sorani_Kurdish_Morphology

The manuscript states that it was still being completed and discusses Sorani subdialect variation. It is supporting evidence, not final authority. The exact Kurdish-script form «نەدەنووسی» still requires independent specialist review.
