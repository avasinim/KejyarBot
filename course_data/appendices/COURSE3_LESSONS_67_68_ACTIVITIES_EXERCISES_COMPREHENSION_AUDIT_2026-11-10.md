## Decision key
- **KEEP** — instruction is sufficiently clear and can be answered without asserting an unverified linguistic rule.
- **REVISE** — retain the pedagogical goal, but narrow the wording, add context, or specify the expected evidence.
- **HOLD** — do not score or teach as a settled rule until the exact Sorani form/lexeme is verified from a dialect-specific source.
- **OPEN TASK** — keep as a productive/open-ended task; do not force a single answer key.
