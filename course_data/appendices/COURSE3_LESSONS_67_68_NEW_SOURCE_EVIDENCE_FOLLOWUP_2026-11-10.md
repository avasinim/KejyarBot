- keep the compound segmentation and any scored morphological claim on HOLD;

## 2. Lesson 68 — negative past progressive: the Sorani pattern is attested, but the exact lesson form still needs checking

Sina Ahmadi (2021), *A Formal Description of Sorani Kurdish Morphology*, provides an explicit rule for the **past progressive of intransitive verbs**:
- Rule 7.9 identifies ne- as a negation prefix and de-/e- as a modal prefix.
- It gives heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe → hełnedehatînewe, translated as “(we) were not fleeing away.”
- Lines 2493–2532 of the ResearchGate-hosted manuscript: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354494225_A_Formal_Description_of_Sorani_Kurdish_Morphology

The same manuscript's **past progressive of transitive verbs** (Rule 7.10) identifies the modal prefix de-/e-, agent/person prefix material, and ne- as the negative prefix, with the example ne-yan-de-kêł-a → neyandekêła, translated as “(they) were not ploughing” (lines 2533–2577).

A second Sorani morphology-generation paper by Aziz, Teimoor, Toq and Salih (2023), DOI https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-2748581/v1, describes negative simple past and past-continuous structures in simple, complex and compound verbs; see section 3.4, especially lines 249–263: https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2748581/v1_covered.pdf

**Decision update:** the broad Sorani pattern ne- + de-/e- + verbal material in past-progressive constructions has now been evidenced. The original «نەدەنووسی» form should nevertheless remain held as an exact scored example until a specialist checks its intended verb stem, person/agreement, transitivity, dialect and standard Kurdish-script spelling. Do not teach the one intransitive example as a universal template for every verb class.

## 3. Important source-quality caveats
- Ahmadi's manuscript itself says it was still being completed and invites correction; it is a valuable formal description but not an unquestionable final reference. The paper explicitly discusses Sorani subdialect variation.
- The Aziz et al. paper is a 2023 preprint/research manuscript focused on computational generation; its formal descriptions should be triangulated with descriptive grammars and native-speaker review.
- Do not use Haig's Kurmanji chapter as evidence for Sorani. The search result for Haig's Kurmanji grammar discusses a different dialect and must remain excluded from the Sorani evidence chain.
- Do not infer the exact form «نەدەنووسی» solely from a similar ne-de- construction in another verb. The full paradigm and person marking matter.

## 6. Further exact-form and lexical search findings

### Exact surface attestation of «نەدەنووسی»
A published Sorani-script text by Nawa Mukerji, *ئولیس و ڕاڤەکانی* (Volume I, 2025), includes the phrase «پێشتر هەرگیز ... نەدەنووسی» in an editorial note. The searchable PDF passage is at the source's lines 892–900:
https://nawa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/01-Kurdish-Ulysses-With-Annotations-Vol-I-21-Mar-2025.pdf

This is a direct surface-form attestation in Sorani prose. It improves the exact-form status of «نەدەنووسی» beyond a merely constructed example. The surrounding meaning is approximately “previously I never used to write [them]”; however, this editorial prose does not itself provide a formal morpheme segmentation or label the category as past progressive versus past habitual. Combine this attestation with Ahmadi's formal ne- + de-/e- past-progressive patterns, but keep the exact grammatical label and person/stem segmentation subject to specialist confirmation.

**Disposition refinement:** «نەدەنووسی» is no longer unsupported as a surface form. It can be used in a contextual reading/attestation exercise with the cited sentence. Keep a scored morphology question on HOLD until the exact grammatical analysis, target variety and answer wording are checked.

### «بەدخواز»: found in a Hewrami dictionary, not admissible as Sorani evidence
The term appears in *فەرهەنگی کوردی هەورامان* (Ali Newzad, 2005), with a gloss and segmentation «بەد + خواز»: https://www.kurdi.info/ferheng/herman1.pdf
The PDF itself identifies the work as a Hewrami Kurdish dictionary at its opening pages. Therefore this record must **not** be used to establish Sorani lexical status or productivity. It is evidence for a Hewrami dictionary entry only. A Sorani-specific source is still required before using «بەدخواز» as a scored Sorani example.

### «چاونەشۆر»: occurrence as a personal epithet is not a lexical definition
A text listing personal nicknames includes «برایم چاونەشۆر» among examples of personal descriptions/nicknames: https://mitania.net/?p=966
This occurrence is not a dictionary headword or a clear definition of the common noun/adjective intended in the lesson. Keep the lesson's proposed lexical analysis on HOLD until a Sorani dictionary/corpus example in a full sentence is found.
