## 3. Redundancy findings
The 12 original items overrepresent three targets:
- Negative imperative: Q02, Q04, Q08.
- Negative non-verbal predicate «ئازا نییە»: Q06, Q09.
- Lexical «بێ-»: Q05, Q10.
- Negative indicative/present: Q01, Q12.

The revised bank improves conceptual coverage by adding:
- negative subjunctive vs negative indicative (V3-Q02);
- source-scoped negative past (V3-Q04);
- no dedicated morphological future marker in Haig's account (V3-Q05);
- lexicalized «تێرنەخۆر» without claiming productive prefixation (V3-Q08);
- dialect/source-scope reasoning (V3-Q09);
- a bounded Ahmadi past-progressive source-reading item (V3-Q10).

V4 has 10 items: V3-Q01–05 and V3-Q07–10, with V4-Q06 replacing V3-Q06. The answer key change is limited to the revised negative-predicate question: V4-Q06 answer C. This is the proposal's internal key, not an active course key.

A balanced future 10-item bank can use the following distinct targets:
1. Negative indicative (source-labelled).
2. Negative subjunctive (source-labelled).
3. Negative imperative (source-labelled).
4. Negative past in full source context.
