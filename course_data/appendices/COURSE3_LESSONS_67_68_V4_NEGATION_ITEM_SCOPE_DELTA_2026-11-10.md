# COURSE 3 — V4 NEGATION ITEM SCOPE DELTA
Date: 2026-11-10
Status: append-only clarification for V4 question 68-V3-Q10 and Lesson 68 source framing.

## Why this delta is needed
The newly consolidated dialect evidence shows that Sorani negative morphology cannot be represented by a single undifferentiated table. Haig (2018) explicitly labels his present-stem forms as Suleimaniya Sorani and documents dialectal variation in the affirmative indicative prefix. Tabei et al. (2024) describe a distinct Ardalani negation system with four markers and aspect-dependent asymmetry. Ahmadi (2021) provides the specific «ne- + de-/e-» past-progressive analyses in Rules 7.9 and 7.10, but his manuscript notes that it was still being completed.

## V4 question 68-V3-Q10 — retain with a precise scope
The existing question asks specifically about Ahmadi's cited form:
«heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe»

The keyed answer B (ne- precedes de-) remains correct **within the segmentation and terminology of Ahmadi Rule 7.9**. Do not rewrite it as a universal Sorani past-progressive rule.

### Terminology caveat
The V4 explanation calls de-/e- a "modal prefix", following Ahmadi's terminology. Keep this attribution explicit:
- Safe: "Ahmadi (2021), in Rule 7.9, labels de-/e- as a modal prefix in this formal analysis."
- Unsafe: "de-/e- is universally the modal prefix for Sorani past progressive."
Haig (2018) describes e- as the past-imperfective marker in his Suleimaniya-oriented account and reports e-/de- as dialectal variants of the present indicative prefix. These are analyses in different frameworks and constructions; do not silently treat the labels as interchangeable.

## Lesson 68 claims — scope corrections
1. Keep the present indicative nā-/na:-, subjunctive ne-/næ-, imperative me-/mæ- and negative copular ni:- in separate source- and dialect-labelled rows.
2. Do not merge Ardalani ni:- (reported as restricted to present «bûn») with every orthographic instance of «نییە» in all Sorani varieties.
3. Do not present continuous negation as a single concatenative rule. The Ardalani study reports asymmetric strategies in imperfective continuous and modal structures.
4. Keep «نەدەنووسی» as surface-attested only; no exact segmentation is licensed by the two past-progressive examples in Ahmadi.
5. Keep the six-person «ناخوێنم» paradigm held until a dialect-specific source or specialist-checked elicitation is available.
