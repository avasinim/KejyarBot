## Additional results

### «گوڵەباخ»
The exact headword is present in Central Kurdish Wiktionary, but the available entry labels it as a **proper noun**, the name of a neighbourhood in Sulaymaniyah; the etymology section is empty and the entry is marked as a stub:
https://ckb.wiktionary.org/wiki/گوڵەباخ
A contemporary dictionary also records «ڕۆنی گوڵەباخ» as rose oil / rose water, but this is an embedded compound phrase and does not establish «گوڵەباخ» as a common noun with the intended meaning in the lesson:
https://ferheng.info/alfba/17454/
**Decision:** continue to HOLD the original lesson's analysis of «گوڵەباخ» as a grammatical/compound example. The proper-name entry cannot validate the lesson's intended lexical analysis. Ask for the intended meaning and source context before replacing it.

### «بێهۆش»
VejînLex search results expose an entry for the verbal phrase «بێهۆش بوون» in *Henbane Borine* (Hajar, 1990), glossed «لە هۆش چوون، بوورانەوە»:
https://lex.vejin.net/ck/def/henbaneborine/بێهۆش%20بوون
A separate historical lexicographic PDF also records the expression «بێهۆش بون» among glosses for losing consciousness:
https://kurdishlo.org/pdf/dictionary/Ferhengi_Hejir_1.pdf
**Decision:** the expression «بێهۆش بوون» is attested. This does not by itself establish every syntactic use of bare «بێهۆش» as an adjective or prove that «بێ-» is freely productive with arbitrary roots. Change the teaching note from “no direct evidence for the expression” to “phrase attested; adjective classification requires context.”

### «بەدشکڵ»
Further results show:
- *Ferhengî Merdox* is cited under «بەد چێره» with «بەدشکڵ» as a synonym/variant: https://bradost.net/ferheng/index.php?name=چێ
- The form «بەدشکڵ» also appears in a Kurdish literary text preserved by Kurdipedia: https://www.kurdipedia.org/default.aspx?lng=5&q=20221006001629438071
**Decision:** this is stronger than a bare unverified search result, but still not a fully inspected standalone headword with dialect-labelled segmentation. Keep the form as provisionally attested; do not use it to prove productive derivation by «بەد-».

### «چاونەشۆر» and «بەدخواز»
No direct, reliable, dialect-labelled dictionary entry or corpus example was located in this follow-up search. This remains **not verified in this search**, not proof of non-existence.
**Decision:** HOLD pending a direct source or a supplied sentence with a clear meaning and regional variety.
