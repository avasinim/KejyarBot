## Evidence base and limits
1. Geoffrey Haig (2018), “The Iranian languages of northern Iraq,” in *The Languages and Linguistics of Western Asia*, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110421682-009. Relevant Sorani sections distinguish `nā-` negative indicative, `ne-` negative subjunctive/past contexts, `me-` negative imperative, the 3SG copula `=e`, the directional clitic `=(y)e`, and the definite suffix `-eke`.
2. Sina Ahmadi (2021), *A Formal Description of Sorani Kurdish Morphology*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.03942. Useful as supporting descriptive evidence; it should not be treated as a final authority for every disputed construction.
3. Sadaf Kalami (2023), “Nominal linkers in Central Kurdish (Ardalāni variety),” *Toronto Working Papers in Linguistics* 45(1), DOI: https://doi.org/10.33137/twpl.v45i1.41691. Relevant to Ezafe/linking forms, but the Ardalani variety must not be silently generalized to all Sorani varieties.
