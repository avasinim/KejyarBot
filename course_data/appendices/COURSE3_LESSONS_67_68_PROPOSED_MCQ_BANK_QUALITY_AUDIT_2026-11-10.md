# COURSE 3 — PROPOSED MCQ BANK QUALITY AUDIT
Date: 2026-11-10
Scope: proposed revised MCQ bank for lessons 67–68, checked against the 24-item source quiz audit and the candidate lesson content.
Status: append-only QA report; no candidate lesson, quiz, answer key or runtime file changed.

## 1. Overall finding
The first proposed bank contains 20 items (9 for Lesson 67 and 11 for Lesson 68), but it is not yet a high-quality final bank. The answer keys are internally consistent with the written options, but several items test the same learning objective more than once, some distractors are too easy, and correct answers are heavily concentrated in option A/B. The bank must remain a proposal.

## 3. Linguistic/source validation

### Strongly supported by Haig (2018), Sorani section
- Sorani has a definite suffix -eke; see chapter section 3.2, lines 177–212 and Table 2.
- Haig distinguishes phrasal Ezafe =ī from compound Ezafe =e; see lines 250–280.
- In the Sulemaniyya Sorani paradigm, nā- marks negative indicative, ne- marks negative subjunctive, and me- marks negative imperative; Table 6, lines 374–413.
- Haig explicitly says Sorani lacks a dedicated future marker and uses the present in future contexts; lines 421–430.
- Haig gives ne-kušt “I didn't kill the dog” as a negative past example; lines 539–544.
- Haig distinguishes copular clitic =e (Table 5, lines 314–371) from directional particle =(y)e attached to motion verbs; lines 473–487.

### Required qualifications
- Haig's detailed morphosyntax overview is based primarily on Sulemaniyya Sorani, and the chapter explicitly notes dialect variation. Label examples/source scope; do not imply every form is identical in all Central Kurdish varieties.
- ne-kušt is a past-stem negative transitive example with person-clitic placement complexity. It is a valid source example, but an introductory quiz should avoid implying that the whole morphology of transitive past verbs is simple.
- The source supports the function of =e and =(y)e as different constructions; it does not mean every written Kurdish «ە» maps one-to-one onto either clitic.
- The proposed spelling «-ەکە/-eke» should be labelled as an orthographic/dialect mapping rather than a universal phonological equivalence.
- The phrase «ئەو ڕۆیشتووە» remains under review. Haig describes participles combined with copular person markers, but the exact analysis of this particular Kurdish-script form must be validated for the target dialect before it becomes a scored parsing question.
- «ڕەشەبا» has lexical attestation, but its internal segmentation remains unverified. Keep that morphology out of scored items.
- «نەدەنووسی» remains HOLD; no new quiz item should assess its segmentation until a direct Sorani source confirms the exact form.

## 4. Answer-key and assessment design findings
- Every listed answer letter in the first proposal corresponds to the correct option text as written.
- Correct answer positions are not sufficiently balanced; most items use A or B, making pattern guessing easier.
- Several distractors are implausibly unrelated to the question, especially in the abstract =e / =(y)e item.
- A scored item should have one defensible answer, source/dialect scope where relevant, and distractors representing realistic learner confusions.
- The first proposal says 4 HOLD items are excluded, but the retained/replacement IDs do not map cleanly to the original quiz IDs. A future integration must preserve an explicit source-ID → replacement-ID mapping.
- The 20-item bank must not be treated as a finalized 24-item replacement. The original 24 questions and keys remain preserved separately.

## 5. Corrective action
A separate version-2 proposal should:
1. Remove repeated learning objectives.
2. Use source-example-based questions for Ezafe, copula, direction and negation.
3. Balance answer positions across A/B/C.
4. Use plausible distractors that test likely confusions, not obviously irrelevant categories.
5. Keep disputed items (the internal morphology of «ڕەشەبا», the phrase «ئەو ڕۆیشتووە», «کچە جوانەکە», and «نەدەنووسی») out of scored assessment until specialist review.
6. Include a transparent mapping to the original audit decisions and preserve the original 24 MCQs unchanged.
