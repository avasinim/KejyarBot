## Lesson 67 — «ڕەشەبا»
- Use «ڕەشەبا» in a lexical lookup task: ask for dictionary headword, meaning, dialect/variety, and source.
- VejinLex's *فەرهەنگی شێخانی* (2009) records it as a strong winter wind; *هەنبانە بۆرینە* (Hajar, 1990) records it as a strong westerly wind:
  - https://lex.vejin.net/ck/def/shexani/ڕەشەبا
  - https://lex.vejin.net/ck/def/henbaneborine/ڕەشەبا
- Do not ask learners to infer internal morphology or etymology from the spelling. Keep segmentation out of scored tasks.

## Lesson 68 — «نەدەنووسی» and past-progressive negation
- A published Sorani-script prose source (Nawa Mukerji, *ئولیس و ڕاڤەکانی*, Vol. I, 2025) contains «پێشتر هەرگیز ... نەدەنووسی» (PDF lines 892–900): https://nawa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/01-Kurdish-Ulysses-With-Annotations-Vol-I-21-Mar-2025.pdf
- Ahmadi's Sorani morphology manuscript Rule 7.9 gives the intransitive past-progressive example heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe and explicitly identifies ne- as negation and de-/e- as a modal prefix: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354494225_A_Formal_Description_of_Sorani_Kurdish_Morphology
- Therefore, use both sources in a research/reading task: learners locate the surface form in prose, compare it with the formal example, and state what is established and what still needs a specialist's analysis.
- Do not force a one-to-one gloss or full segmentation of «نەدەنووسی» until the exact person, verb stem, aspect/habitual reading and variety are reviewed.
- V3 MCQ Q10 uses the cited Ahmadi example and asks only about the order/function of ne- and de-. It does not assert that the example is the exact equivalent of «نەدەنووسی».

## Lexical forms not yet approved for scored Sorani work
