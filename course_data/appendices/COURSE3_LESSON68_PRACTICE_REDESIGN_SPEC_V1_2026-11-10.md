## 2. Evidence policy for teaching and scoring
### 2.1 Source-scoped examples currently safe to teach analytically
**Haig (2018), Suleimaniya-oriented Sorani description, Table 6:**
- e-łē-m — “I say / am saying” (affirmative indicative).
- nā-łē-m — “I don't say” (negative indicative).
- bi-łē-m — “that I say” (affirmative subjunctive).
- ne-łē-m — “that I not say” (negative subjunctive).
- bi-łē / bi-łē-n — affirmative imperative (2SG/2PL).
- me-łē / me-łē-n — negative imperative (2SG/2PL).

Use these as source-transcribed examples with a clear label “Haig's Suleimaniya Sorani table”. Do not silently convert their Latin transcription into Kurdish script as if the mapping were independently verified. A Kurdish-script classroom equivalent needs specialist orthographic confirmation.

**Haig (2018), past examples:**
- min seg-eke=m ne-kušt — “I didn't kill the dog.”
- Past imperfective is described as past stem plus e- in this analysis. Keep this separate from the present-stem indicative table.

**Ahmadi (2021), selected past-progressive examples:**
- heł-ne-de-hat-în-ewe — “(we) were not fleeing away” (Rule 7.9, intransitive).
- ne-yan-de-kêł-a — “(they) were not ploughing” (Rule 7.10, transitive).
These are bounded examples in a manuscript/preprint; do not use them to generate a six-person paradigm or a universal formula.

**Tabei et al. (2024), Ardalani Sorani:**
- The article reports næ-, na:-, ni:- and mæ- and construction-specific asymmetry. Teach this as a separate dialect comparison, not as an interchangeable spelling set for the Suleimaniya table.

### 2.2 Distinctions mandatory in every answer key
1. Negative indicative ≠ negative subjunctive.
2. Negative imperative ≠ negative indicative.
3. Negative copular/non-verbal predicate «نییە» ≠ verbal prefix «ne-/nā-/me-» by default.
4. Clause-level negation ≠ lexical word formation.
5. Lexicalized dictionary item ≠ proof of productive prefixation.
6. Past simple ≠ past imperfective ≠ past progressive.
7. Dialect notation/transcription ≠ Kurdish orthographic spelling without a verified mapping.

## 3. Proposed learning sequence
### 68-A — What is being negated?
Learner identifies scope:
- whole clause / finite verb;
- imperative command;
- non-verbal predicate with negative copula;
- lexical item formed or stored as a word.

Core safe contrast:
- nā-łē-m — clause/verb-level negative indicative in Haig's Suleimaniya table.
- me-łē — negative imperative in that same table.
- «بێسوود» — lexical formation; keep only if dictionary headword and spelling are cited in the classroom edition.
- «ئازا نییە» — sentence-level negative predicate, with the exact analysis of «نییە» kept at the construction level unless a dialect-specific morphology source is supplied.

### 68-B — Present-stem mood contrasts (Suleimaniya-oriented)
Teach the six forms from Haig Table 6 as a comparison table. Learners label indicative, subjunctive, imperative and the corresponding negative form. Use exact source transliteration in the source column and avoid unverified Kurdish-script conversion.

### 68-C — Past forms
Teach the Haig negative past example with its full clause and translation. State that it is one attested example and not a full person paradigm. Keep past imperfective separate.

### 68-D — Past progressive: bounded source reading
Learners inspect the two Ahmadi examples and answer only:
- which segment Ahmadi identifies as the negator;
- which segment follows it in the cited segmentation;
- whether the example is marked intransitive or transitive by the source;
- what the source translation says.
Do not ask them to transform «دەنووسی» or «دەکرد» into a complete paradigm.

### 68-E — Dialect comparison
Use a comparison worksheet for Haig's Suleimaniya-oriented description and Tabei et al.'s Ardalani article. Learners compare the inventory and identify why one variety's form should not automatically be generalized to another. Do not require a single Kurdish-script form for the Ardalani transliteration.

### 68-F — Lexical negation and lexicalization
Use dictionary-verified headwords only. «تێرنەخۆر» can be included only with its exact dictionary entry and explanation that it is lexicalized, not evidence of free productive negation. Keep «بەدخواز» out of Sorani scored content because the located evidence was Hewrami, not Sorani. Keep «چاونەشۆر» held because the located occurrence was an epithet/name, not a verified lexical definition.
