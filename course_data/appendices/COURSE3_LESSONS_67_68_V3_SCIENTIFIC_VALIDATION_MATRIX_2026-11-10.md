## Review standard
Each item is checked for (1) one defensible keyed answer, (2) fit between wording and evidence, (3) dialect/source scope, (4) distractor quality, and (5) preservation of unresolved source claims. "KEEP" means the current proposed item is acceptable within its stated scope; it does not mean the original lesson claim is universally established. "REVISE" means wording or explanation should be tightened before scoring. "HOLD" means no scored use until evidence or specialist review is supplied.
