# COURSE 3 — DIALECT-SPECIFIC LEXICAL EVIDENCE ADDENDUM
Date: 2026-11-10
Scope: lexical evidence relevant to Lessons 67–68, collected after the 62-item practice audit.
Status: append-only research addendum; it does not overwrite the earlier audit or alter lesson content.

## Method and confidence labels
- **Confirmed lexical attestation**: a dictionary/lexicographic resource gives the headword or an explicit form and meaning.
- **Attested in a scholarly analysis**: a peer-reviewed/academic discussion gives the form and its morphological/semantic analysis; dialect and method caveats remain.
- **Partial/secondary attestation**: appears as a synonym, variant, or in a multi-dialect cross-reference, not as a fully documented headword.
- **Not verified in this search**: no sufficiently reliable direct entry was located. This is not proof that the word does not exist.
- Lexical attestation does not by itself prove that a prefix is productive, that the form is standard in every Sorani subdialect, or that the proposed segmentation is synchronically transparent.

## New findings

### 1. «ڕەشەبا»
**Status: confirmed as a Sorani lexical item; internal compound analysis remains unproven.**
- VejînLex's entry for Abdulwaهاب Sheikhani's *Ferhengî Şêxanî* (2009) gives «ڕەشەبا» with the definition «بارەش» and describes it as a strong winter wind lasting around seven to ten days: https://lex.vejin.net/ck/def/shexani/ڕەشەبا
- VejînLex's *Henbane Borine* (Hajar, 1990) entry gives «بای توندی ڕۆژاوا»: https://lex.vejin.net/ck/def/henbaneborine/ڕەشەبا
- VejînLex's *Ferhengî Merdox* (1957) gives a west wind, cold in winter and hot in summer: https://lex.vejin.net/en/def/merdox/ڕەشەبا
**Teaching consequence:** remove «ڕەشەبا» from the unverified-lexeme hold list. It is safe to teach its lexical meaning with source attribution. However, do not claim that its written «ە» is a productive morpheme or that its internal structure transparently demonstrates one of the grammatical functions of «ە». Keep the morphological segmentation question on HOLD.

### 2. «تێرنەخۆر»
**Status: confirmed lexical entry and explicitly segmented form.**
- *Ferhengî Zimanî Yekgirtûy Kurdî Hawçerx* gives «تێرنەخۆر» and explicitly segments it as «تێر + نە + خۆر», with the meaning «تێرنەبوون». It also gives «تێرنەخۆرە» for a person who remains hungry despite eating: https://ferheng.info/alfba/16649/
- The same entry separately records «تێرنەبوون» as «تێر + نە + بوون».
**Teaching consequence:** this is no longer an unsupported example. It can be used as a lexically attested formation. Still distinguish this lexicalized/word-formation pattern from ordinary clause-level verbal negation. The dictionary's exact grammatical label and dialect scope should be retained when quoting it.

### 3. «نەزان»
**Status: confirmed lexical attestation; morphological analysis supported.**
- Wîkîferheng's Sorani–Kurmanji lexical appendix lists «نەزان» (nezan) with the meaning “a person who does not know [something]”: https://ku.wiktionary.org/wiki/Pêvek:soranî-kurmancî/Ceribandin
- Fatah & Hamawand (2014), “A Prototype Approach to Kurdish Prefixes,” explicitly analyze *nezan* as ne- + verbal root zan, with the sense “ignorant”: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279191847_A_Prototype_Approach_to_Kurdish_Prefixes
**Teaching consequence:** retain «نەزان» as an attested lexical example, but do not imply that every verb root can take نە- to create a standard adjective. Productivity is limited and lexical evidence remains necessary.

### 4. «ناخۆش» and «نەخۆش»
**Status: both forms attested; their semantic distinction must be contextualized, not flattened.**
- Wîkîferheng's Sorani–Kurmanji appendix lists both «ناخۆش» (naxoş) and «نەخۆش» (nexoş), with overlapping glosses including nexweş/nesax: https://ku.wiktionary.org/wiki/Pêvek:soranî-kurmancî/Ceribandin
- Fatah & Hamawand (2014) distinguish the derivational readings in their analysis: ne-xoŝ is glossed “ill” from xoŝ “healthy”; na-xoŝ is glossed “uncomfortable” from xoŝ “comfortable”. This is an academic analysis based partly on native-speaker feedback and should not be treated as the only possible dialect-wide semantic distinction: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279191847_A_Prototype_Approach_to_Kurdish_Prefixes
**Teaching consequence:** retain both spellings as attested, but revise the exercise to ask for context-specific meanings and source evidence. Do not mark them as freely interchangeable or assign a universal one-word gloss without dialect/context.

### 5. «بێسوود»
**Status: partial lexicographic attestation.**
- The contemporary unified Kurdish dictionary has an entry under «سوود» for «بێسوود/bêsûd», segmented «بێ + سوود» and glossed as useless/without benefit: https://ferheng.info/alfba/28021/
**Teaching consequence:** suitable as an example of privative «بێ-» with a cited dictionary entry. Before making it a canonical Sorani paradigm example, verify the dictionary's dialect label and corroborate with a Sorani-specific source.

### 6. «بەدشکڵ»
**Status: secondary/partial attestation.**
- A Bradost dictionary search result cites *Ferhengî Merdox* under «بەد چێره» and lists «بەدشکڵ» among synonymous forms: https://bradost.net/ferheng/index.php?name=چێ
- This is not yet a standalone, fully inspected headword entry for «بەدشکڵ».
**Teaching consequence:** do not use it as the sole proof of a productive «بەد-» negative prefix. It can remain a provisional synonym pending a direct headword entry and clear segmentation.

### 7. «گوڵەباخ»، «بەدخواز»، «چاونەشۆر»، «بێهۆش»
**Status: not verified in this search.**
- Search did not produce a reliable, direct Sorani dictionary headword for these exact forms.
- This is a search limitation, not proof of non-existence.
**Teaching consequence:** keep these examples on HOLD until a direct dictionary entry, corpus citation, or documented native usage is collected. For «بێهۆش», separately check whether the intended form is «بێهۆش» or «بێ‌هۆش» and whether the source treats it as an adjective, noun, or lexicalized expression.

## Source quality and limitations
1. **VejînLex entries** reproduce identifiable dictionary sources and are useful for lexical attestation. Their cited source, dialect, publication year, and exact definition should be recorded, rather than citing the aggregator alone.
2. **Ferheng.info** is a contemporary lexicographic resource. Its pages can combine multiple dialect labels and older dictionary references; check the headword's dialect/abbreviation key before generalizing to Sorani.
3. **Wîkîferheng's Sorani–Kurmanji appendix** is a cross-reference list, not a complete independent monolingual Sorani dictionary. It is useful as a discovery/corroboration resource but should be paired with another dictionary or corpus.
4. **Fatah & Hamawand (2014)** is relevant to Kurdish negative-prefix semantics, but it notes that native-speaker feedback contributed to the analysis. Treat it as scholarly evidence, not as a universal rule for every dialect and register.
5. **Ahmadi (2020), HUNSPELL for Sorani Kurdish Spell Checking and Morphological Analysis** reports a 23,223-entry morphosyntactically tagged lexicon assembled from Wîkîferheng and FreeDict and warns that Kurdish dialectal/orthographic variation complicates lexical validation: https://sinaahmadi.github.io/docs/articles/ahmadi2020Hunspell.pdf
6. **Bharati et al. (2026), Morphological Feature Extraction for Fine-Grained Sorani Kurdish Dialect Identification**, ACL Anthology, documents six Sorani varieties and distinctions such as -aka (Iraqi Sorani) vs -eke (Iranian Sorani), and de- vs a- present habitual forms: https://aclanthology.org/2026.abjadnlp-1.24.pdf. This supports explicit dialect labelling, not lexical definitions of the target items.

## Recommended disposition updates to the earlier 62-item audit
These are evidence-driven recommendations for a future revised patch; the original audit remains preserved unchanged.
- Lesson 67 references to «ڕەشەبا»: remove the HOLD applied to its lexical existence/meaning, but retain HOLD for its internal morphological segmentation.
- Lesson 68 references to «تێرنەخۆر»: remove the HOLD applied solely to lexical existence; keep the pedagogical distinction between lexicalized derivation and clause negation.
- Lesson 68 references to «نەزان»: remove the HOLD applied solely to lexical existence; retain the productivity caveat.
- Lesson 68 «ناخۆش/نەخۆش»: revise rather than hold the pair as a whole; teach only with dialect/context labels and the distinct semantic analysis qualified as one scholarly account.
- «بێسوود»: may be used as a provisional dictionary-backed example; corroborate dialect before treating it as universal Sorani.
- «بەدشکڵ»: partial evidence only; keep as provisional.
- «گوڵەباخ»، «بەدخواز»، «چاونەشۆر»، «بێهۆش»: remain on HOLD pending direct evidence.
