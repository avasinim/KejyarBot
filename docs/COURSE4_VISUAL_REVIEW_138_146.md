## Findings from pp. 138–146
1. The source shifts from the general classification of verbal forms to a dedicated section on **کاری ڕابردوو**.
2. Several named grammarians' analyses are presented independently. The classifications on pp.138–141 are not one uniform paradigm.
3. The source itself warns against transferring Persian grammatical labels directly to Kurdish without checking whether they fit Kurdish structure.
4. The past-tense examples on pp.144–146 are explicitly dialect-sensitive. The labels **زاری کرمانجیی سەروو** and **زاری خوارمانجی** must remain attached to their respective examples; they cannot be generalized to all Kurdish varieties.
5. Person/pronoun elements in past constructions are discussed as part of the grammatical structure. Their position and function need full sentence-level analysis; they should not be simplified into a single suffix rule.
6. Several paradigms are visible but not yet transcribed at character level. No table or rule from this block is approved for direct lesson use.
