## Editorial safeguards

1. Preserve the book's sequence and headings. The proposed units are organizational containers, not a claim that every source chapter is theoretically consistent.
2. Keep **ڕاناو** (p.25) and **چاوگ** (p.222) distinct; they are different source headings.
3. Keep **کار** and **چاوگ** separate where the source does so, and preserve parenthetical grouping where it does so. Do not harmonize the source's variation.
4. Attribute all borrowed taxonomies and comparative passages to their named authors/sources. Do not merge them into one universal Kurdish classification.
5. Preserve **گیرەک** exactly as printed. Do not infer a modern morpheme/clitic category without separate validation.
6. The source is about spoken Kurdish; do not generalize every statement to all Kurdish varieties or registers.
7. Complete exact transcription and independent linguistic validation for each lesson's selected examples before drafting lesson text. Unresolved or contradictory claims are excluded or clearly qualified.
8. The separate PDF **ڕێزمانی ڕاناوی لکاو.pdf** remains excluded from Course 4 content.
