## 2. Cross-source distinctions that must be retained

1. **Different authors, different frameworks:** the multiple accounts on pp.374–385 are not to be merged into a single taxonomy.
2. **Comparative sources remain comparative:** the French discussion on p.381, Persian inventory on pp.384–385, and Evan C. Penglis reference on p.386 must be attributed and not presented as direct unqualified Central Kurdish facts.
3. **Infix remains an open question in the source:** pp.387 and 395 mention Infix, but neither passage alone establishes a productive Kurdish infix category.
4. **Independent/bound framework is distinct:** the five independent and eight bound classes on pp.388–389, the p.392 diagram, the numbered examples on pp.393–394, and the separate account on p.395 are not one interchangeable classification.
5. **Source-internal grouping varies:** p.347 lists **کار** and **چاوگ** separately, while p.373 and pp.388–389 use a parenthetical grouping. Preserve the page-specific evidence; do not harmonize.
6. **Prefix subclasses:** **سادە** and **ناسادە** on p.397 are presented as a form-based distinction in the prefix discussion, not a universal classification for every bound form.
7. **Printed forms and technical spelling:** preserve the source spelling **گیرەک**. Do not silently normalize unclear or variant printed forms.
