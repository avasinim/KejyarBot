- Render the written wordmark **کەژیار** at the beginning of every Course 3 lesson.
- Keep the lesson number/title immediately below the wordmark.
- Use clear section labels for the objective, key points, examples, exercises, comprehension questions, and quiz.

Apply this checklist to all 66 lessons. This is a text and editorial check, not an image-production task.
