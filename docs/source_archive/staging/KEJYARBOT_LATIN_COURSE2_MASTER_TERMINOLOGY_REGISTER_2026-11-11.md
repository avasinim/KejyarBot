- The **preferred course form** records the form currently used as the headword or teaching label in the reviewed draft. It does not claim that no dialectal or scholarly alternative exists.

- **Status: stable** means preserve this distinction in subsequent editing. **Review** means a language editor or source-based decision is still needed before normalization.
- Do not use global find/replace on grammatical terms. Surface similarity is not proof of identical function.
- The original source-aligned material remains authoritative for source quotations; this register is an editorial navigation aid.

## Lessons 7–8 adjudication addendum — broad affix vs specific determiner (2026-11-11)

A focused review of Lessons 4–8 and the comparative suffix example in Lesson 12 resolves the working relationship between the terms at the course level:
- `pêvek` is the broad morphological term for an affix/suffix attached to a base; it is used for more than one kind of bound morphology (including the comparative `-tir`).
- `veqetandek` is the course's specific label for the definite and indefinite noun-determining forms taught in Lessons 7–8. Preserve `veqetandeka binavkirî` and `veqetandeka nebinavkirî` as stable paradigm labels.
- `pêveka raveker` is descriptive wording for an affix/suffix in a modifier-following environment; it is not a synonym that should replace the formal paradigm label.

Therefore, do not treat `pêvek` and `veqetandek` as interchangeable in headings, glossary definitions, or quiz answers. This is an internal course terminology adjudication, not a claim that every Kurdish grammar tradition uses `veqetandek` identically. A Kurmanji language editor should still confirm the formal glossary wording against the cited edition before it is promoted as an authoritative definition. No lesson/quiz/key text was normalized. ## Source-page refinement — `veqetandek` category vs `pêvek` realization (2026-11-11)

The direct source-page review for §§61–64 (printed pp.62–63) refines the definition above: `veqetandek` is the grammatical determiner category/function, defined by the source as a small word placed before or after a noun to identify/limit reference. Do not define it simply as a suffix. In Lesson 7, `-ê/-a/-ên` are attached after nouns in the main paradigm, while shortened `ê/a/ên` also occur before adjectives when the noun is understood/omitted. Lesson 8 teaches several syntax-conditioned indefinite forms and positions. `pêvek` is the broader morphological term for an affix/suffix and may describe a particular bound realization, but it is not interchangeable with the determiner category `veqetandek`.

**Preferred glossary framing:** `veqetandek` = determiner (grammatical function/category); `pêvek` = affix/suffix (morphological form). Explain the realization and position of each determiner paradigm separately; retain the distinction between post-nominal attached forms and shortened pre-adjectival forms. This is source-aligned for the reviewed sections; the broader use of these terms across all Kurdish grammar traditions is not asserted.

## Lessons 6, 8 and 12 adjudication — noun modifier vs adjective vs object (2026-11-11)

A focused comparison of the source-aligned Lessons 6, 8 and 12 establishes three separate levels:
- `bireserê navdêrê`: broad syntactic noun-attached modifier/complement function; it may be realized by different word classes or phrases and is not itself a lexical word class.
- `rengdêr`: adjective, a word class. Lesson 12 names demonstrative, numeral, interrogative, indefinite, descriptive, and possessive adjective classes. An adjective may realize the noun-modifying function, but not every noun complement is an adjective.
- bare `bireser`: clause-level object role; do not use it as an abbreviation for `bireserê navdêrê` where ambiguity results.

Specific internal consistency: classify `ev` in `ev keçik` as a demonstrative adjective/modifier under Lesson 12's own `rengdêrên îşarkî` framework; classify `Ev` in `Ev hat.` as an independent demonstrative pronoun. Avoid the unexplained Lesson 6 slash-pair `pêşnavdêr/bireser`; do not assert a definitive technical definition of `pêşnavdêr` without primary-source confirmation. In Lesson 8, do not present `bireser/rengdêr` as interchangeable labels: distinguish the broad following complement/modifier environment from the narrower adjective class.
