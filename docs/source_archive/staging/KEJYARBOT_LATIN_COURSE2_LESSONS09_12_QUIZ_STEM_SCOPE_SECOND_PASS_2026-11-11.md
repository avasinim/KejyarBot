## Lesson 9 — 5 items

- Q1–Q4 ask for the inflected-agent forms in the transitive-past paradigm; the forms `min/te/wî/me` are directly listed in the lesson table and exemplified.
- Q5 asks for the second-person plural personal pronoun; `hon` is explicitly stated in the lesson as the course form. Key A is supported.
- The lesson explicitly warns that the table label “forma kirarî” names a paradigm form and does not mean that the form is the clause agent in every sentence. The five quiz items specify the transitive-past paradigm, so this warning is not contradicted.
- No new hold.

## Lesson 10 — 5 items

- Q1 remains held: `Kîjan yek ji pronavên bireser e?` can be read as asking actual clause-object function, while the intended answer `min` is membership in the named paradigm. The lesson itself demonstrates role reversal in transitive past.
- Q2 remains held: `Forma bireserî ya ew mê çi ye?` key C (`wê`) is supported as the feminine paradigm form, but “bireserî” may be read as clause-object function; in `Wê ew dît`, `wê` is the inflected agent and `ew` the direct object. The dedicated L10 Q2 audit proposes explicitly naming the table/paradigm.
- Q3 asks for the two reciprocal-pronoun forms listed in the lesson; key A is supported.
- Q4 explicitly asks for the role of `ew` in `Wan ew xwar`; key B (direct object) is supported by the sentence. It does not have the same paradigm-label ambiguity as Q1/Q2.
- Q5 matches the lesson's scoped definition of reciprocal pronouns in this course; key B is supported by the course's own framing around two or three participants.
- No new hold beyond Q1 and Q2.

## Lesson 11 — 5 items

- Q1–Q3 align with the lesson's demonstrative pronoun forms `ev/ew`, the additional forms `yê/ya/yên`, and the course-preserved relative-pronoun form `ko`.
- Q4 explicitly uses `Min got ko ez nayêm`; the lesson distinguishes relative-pronoun `ko` from conjunction `ko`, and this sentence is the conjunction use. Key B (`gihanek`) is supported.
- Q5 asks what `kîjan` is used for; key C matches the lesson's explanation of selection among alternatives.
- No new hold.

## Lesson 12 — 5 items

- Q1 remains held: the broad stem `Rengdêr çi dike?` asks about the entire adjective category, while key B describes the prototypical/property-describing function of the `wesfîn` subtype. Suggested narrowing to `Rengdêra wesfîn çi dike?` remains a proposal only.
- Q2 correctly identifies `mezin` in `hespê mezin` as a qualitative adjective; key C is supported.
- Q3 remains held only for unresolved morphology of the option label `rengdêra jmarîn` vs `rengdêrê jmarîn`; the category identification and key A are supported by `sê mal` under the lesson's numeral-adjective section. Native-Kurmanji adjudication is still needed.
- Q4 matches the lesson's explicit decision to present `jmarnav` as a separate grammatical class; key B is supported within this teaching framework.
- Q5 asks for the class of `nîv` in the lesson's numeral section; key C is supported. The rationale correctly distinguishes word-class membership from phrase-level numeral function.
- No new hold beyond Q1 and Q3.
