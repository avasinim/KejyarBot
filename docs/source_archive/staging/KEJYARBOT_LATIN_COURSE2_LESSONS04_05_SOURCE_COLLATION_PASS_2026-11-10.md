## Lesson 4 — Mêjer
Printed p.55 §§47–50 was checked against the PDF page text:
- §48: `Mîr hat`, `Ez hespekî dibînim`, `Ez çiyayê bilind dibînim` are singular examples.
- §49: `Mîr hatin`, `Ez hespina dibînim`, `Ez çiyayên bilind dibînim` are plural examples.
- §50 gives distinct paired forms, including `Ev mehîn e / Ev mehîn in`, `Min mehîn dît / Min mehîn dîtin`, `Ez mehîne dibînim / Ez mehînan dibînim`, and `Ez mehînekê dibînim / Ez mehînina dibînim`.
- The reviewed draft had incorrectly paired `Ez mehînekê dibînim` with `Ez mehînan dibînim`, combining two separate paradigms. Corrected by adding the separate source-matching pair `mehîne / mehînan` and `mehînekê / mehînina`.
- Source distinctions between plural marking, oblique inflection and the indefinite forms are retained; the draft's broader `mehînek/mehînin` explanation remains a teaching generalization and should not be mistaken for a verbatim source example.

## Lesson 5 — Tewanga Navdêran
Printed pp.57–59 §§52–58 were checked:
- Source p.57 gives `Ez hespî dibînim / Ez hespan dibînim` and `Ez mehînê dibînim / Ez mehînan dibînim`.
- Source p.59 §57 confirms the three variants `Soro kevirî tavêje`, `Soro kêvir tavêje`, `Soro kevir tavêje`.
- The reviewed draft's feminine noun base spelling was normalized to `mehîn` in the Lesson 5 paradigm, exercise and quiz (including `Mehîn hat`, `mehînê`, `mehînan`). No global replacement was made outside this lesson.
- The `e/a` alternation examples remain source-based and are not generalized as a single unrestricted rule.
