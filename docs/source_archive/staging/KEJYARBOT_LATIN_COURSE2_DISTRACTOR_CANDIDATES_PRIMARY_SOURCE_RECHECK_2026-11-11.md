## Adjudication of six primary candidates

### L1 Q5 — phrase composition

**Current key:** B, `bi peyhevketina koman`.

**Candidate disposition:** Retain as a viable draft, with a terminology check.

- Candidate A (letters alone), B (groups/phrases), C (repetition of words) are more parallel than the unrelated original options.
- The reviewed lesson's own formulation states that a `qiset` is formed through `peyhevketina komekan`; the answer key B follows that teaching statement.
- The primary PDF's early sections §§13–16 distinguish `kît`, `bêje`, and the parts/functions of a sentence, but this particular short formula is the course's teaching synthesis rather than a verbatim source quotation.
- Before approval, confirm that `koman` is used consistently for the intended constituent level throughout the course. No key issue identified.

### L3 Q5 — grammatical gender of inanimate nouns

**Current key:** C, `zayenda rêzimanî`.

**Candidate disposition:** Retain.

- The lesson explicitly teaches that inanimate nouns have grammatical gender, not biological sex.
- Source §§39–45 (printed pp. 52–53) distinguishes masculine/feminine grammatical gender and notes that noun gender is not reducible to biological sex; §44 gives the meaning-sensitive `dar` example.
- Candidate A (biological sex only) and B (noun number) are distinct neighboring categories; C is the lesson's target concept.
- No key change.

### L4 Q1 — what number marks

**Current key:** B, `yek an gelek`.

**Candidate disposition:** Retain.

- Source §§47–51 (printed pp. 55–56) defines singular/plural by whether one or more entities are represented, and gives the contrast `Mîr hat / Mîr hatin`.
- Candidate A (`zayend`) and C (`tewang`) are distinct grammatical dimensions. They are plausible confusions without being alternative definitions of number.
- No key change.

### L5 Q1 — definition of `tewang`

**Current key:** B, `guherîna forma peyvê`.

**Candidate disposition:** Do not approve the current candidate set unchanged; refine A and C to avoid conceptual overlap.

- Source §52 (printed p. 57) defines inflection through changes in word form according to its position/function in the sentence. §§54–56 give examples, including the transitive past pattern.
- The candidate distractors `guherîna zayenda rêzimanî` and `guherîna mêjera navdêrê` are adjacent grammatical dimensions, but gender and number also condition the forms that undergo inflection (see §55). A learner could reasonably see them as aspects or triggers of inflection rather than cleanly exclusive alternatives.
- **Safer proposed options, preserving key B:**
  - A) `guherîna erkê peyvê bê guherîna forma wê`
  - B) `guherîna forma peyvê li gorî erkê wê`
  - C) `guherîna wateya peyvê bê guherîna forma wê`
- This is a more discriminating contrast between form change, role change without form change, and meaning change. Exact Kurmanji idiom still needs native editorial review.

### L8 Q2 — `hespek hat`

**Current key:** C, `bê hêmaneke raveker a li pey navdêrê`.

**Candidate disposition:** Retain as a viable draft; preserve the exact §66/§67 distinction.

- Source §66 (printed pp. 63–65) states that `-ek/-ek/-in` follow nouns with no following complement/modifier and gives `hespek hat`, `hespin hatin`; §67 gives `-ekî/-eke/-ine` when a complement follows, e.g. `hespekî delal hat`.
- Candidate A contrasts the following descriptive element; B contrasts the oblique object environment; C matches the §66 pattern.
- This is more diagnostic than unrelated distractors. Keep the wording explicit that the qualifying element is **after** the noun.
- No key change.

### L11 Q5 — function of `kîjan`

**Current key:** C, `hilbijartina kes/tişt di nav çend vebijarkan de`.

**Candidate disposition:** Retain, but ensure the lesson's own contrast between `kî/kê`, `çi/çî`, and `kîjan` remains explicit.

- Source §§101–103 (printed pp. 84–85) presents interrogative pronouns `kî/kê`, `çi/çî`, and `kîjik/kîjan`; §103 characterizes the latter as used for selecting among people/things.
- Candidate A/B represent asking about an unspecified person or thing; C targets selection among alternatives.
- This is an appropriate distinction, provided A/B are not described elsewhere in the lesson as interchangeable with `kîjan`.
- No key change.

## Separate L4 Q5 coverage proposal — do not place in Lesson 4 quiz yet

The candidate set proposes:
- Stem: `Cudahiya “hespan” û “hespin” çi ye?`
- B: `hespan` is oblique/inflected; `hespin` is indefinite plural.
- Candidate key B, changing the current key C.

The contrast itself is source-backed:
- §55 (printed pp. 57–58) gives `hespan` in an oblique-object example.
- §66 (printed pp. 63–65) gives `hespin` as indefinite plural in `hespin hatin`.

**Sequencing objection:** In the course structure, Lesson 4 introduces number, while the explicit `tewang` lesson is Lesson 5. The proposed stem and answer require learners to distinguish a number form from an oblique/inflected form before the course has taught the latter concept. Therefore, do not apply this as Lesson 4 Q5. Preserve it as a candidate for a cumulative review after Lesson 5, or redesign Lesson 4 Q5 to remain strictly within number.
