## Lesson 12 — Rengdêr û Jmarnav

### Source-supported
1. The source classifies adjectives in six main groups: descriptive/quality, demonstrative, possessive, interrogative, indefinite, and numeral. The draft's six-part taxonomy broadly matches this classification, while using the teaching labels `wesfîn` and `xwemalîn` for the descriptive and possessive groups.
2. The demonstrative forms and examples `ev/ew`, `vî/vê/wî/wê`, `van/wan` are consistent with the source's demonstrative adjective paradigm. The course correctly distinguishes their pre-nominal adjective use from standalone demonstrative-pronoun use.
3. The interrogative adjective examples `kîjan`, `çend`, and `çi` are consistent with the source's interrogative adjective category.
4. The indefinite adjective examples `tu`, `filan`, `her`, `gelek`, `hevqas`, `hemî`, `zehf`, and `çend` are consistent with the source's indefinite group. Some of these forms can also function pronominally depending on syntax; the draft appropriately signals this.
5. The possessive examples `hespê min`, `mehînên te`, `kitêba vî`, `qelemên wê`, `xaniyê me`, `çiyayê we`, `kurên van`, and `keçên wan` are consistent with the possessive-adjective patterns and the relevant definite markers.
6. The source treats numeral adjectives in two classes: cardinal (`jmarda`) and ordinal (`rêzda`). The draft's cardinal examples `yek, du, sê, çar, deh, sed, hezar` and its ordinal examples are consistent with the source's broad distinction.
7. The separate treatment of numerals as a distinct grammatical category (jmarnav) is also present in the source. Its detailed sections distinguish definite and indefinite numerals and discuss cardinal, ordinal, fractional, and collective forms, as well as simple/compound forms and special behavior of `nîv`, `yek`, and `hezar`. The draft's expanded treatment is therefore directionally source-aligned.

### Precision and completeness notes
1. **Source terminology:** the source's descriptive adjective group is named `rengdêrên çilobûn û çawabûnê`; the draft uses `rengdêrên wesfîn`. This is an editorial terminology choice, not the source's exact label. Preserve the source label at first mention, then introduce `wesfîn` as the course's explanatory equivalent if that is the established cross-course term.
2. **Possessive terminology:** the source calls this group `rengdêrên destdaniyê` and also notes the label `rengdêrên xwedîtiyê`. The draft's `xwemalîn` is a course label. Add the source term at first introduction and state the equivalence explicitly; do not imply `xwemalîn` is a verbatim source term.
3. **Ordinal terminology:** the source's wording is `rengdêrên jmarda` and `rengdêrên rêzda`; the draft uses `rêzîn` in the adjective subsection. This can remain as a teaching label if explicitly mapped to source `rêzda`.
4. **Category boundary:** the draft correctly states that `rengdêrên jmarîn` and `jmarnav` are separate in the course's classification. However, the source's numeral sections are extensive and include overlapping forms. Make the distinction functional: numeral adjective modifies a stated noun; numeral pronoun functions independently or substantively in the sentence. Avoid defining the difference only as “one is a separate section in the book.”
5. **Ordinal examples:** `stûna çaran`, `a pêncan`, and `ê heftan` should be labelled as source examples only where their exact written forms have been checked on the printed page image. In this audit they are treated as examples reported in the draft, not independently re-transcribed quotations.
6. **Degrees of descriptive adjectives:** `mezin / mezintir / herî mezin` is a useful teaching explanation, but it should be marked as an added explanatory grammar point unless its exact treatment is separately located in the cited source section.
7. **Sentence-level numeral examples:** the contrast `deh mirov hatin` / `deh mirovan got` is pedagogically useful for showing case/role variation. Keep it distinct from the cardinal/ordinal taxonomy and ensure the lesson explains that the noun's form changes because of its syntactic role, not because the numeral itself changes.

## Disposition
- Preserve Lesson 12 body, exercises, and answer key unchanged in this pass.

- Strengthen the functional distinction between adjective numerals and independent numeral pronouns.
- Label comparative/superlative degrees as an editorial teaching addition unless a precise source locator is separately verified.
