## Primary source newly inspected

Celadet Alî Bedir-Xan, *Elfabêya kurdî & Bingehên gramera kurdmancî*, Nefel, Stockholm, 2002, ISBN 91-89687-04-3. A full digital copy was inspected at the publisher-edition PDF URL:
`https://kurdshop.net/content/upload/1/root/celadet-ali-bedir-xan-elfabeugramer-02.pdf`

Relevant printed sections/pages:
- §13–15, pp. 43–44: `Kît` and `Bêje`.
- §§59–60, p. 61: `Bireserê navdêrê`.
- §§65–68, pp. 63–65: indefinite determiners, including §66 `EK, EK, IN` and §67 `EKÎ, EKE, INE`.
- §86, p. 76: `Pronavên bireser` and their construction-dependent role change.
- §§119–124, pp. 89–90: adjective definition, six adjective classes and the function of descriptive adjectives.
- §§134–135, p. 94: numeral adjectives and their function.

This pass materially improves the earlier audits: the primary printed source itself is now directly inspectable for these sections. The source is not a substitute for a native editor's judgment on every proposed Kurmanji sentence.

## Item-by-item adjudication and proposed safe resolution

### L1 Q4 — `hesp` as a meaningful unit

- **Key B (`bêje`) remains correct.**
- Source §§13–15 distinguishes `kît` (syllable) from `bêje` (word) and defines the latter as a meaningful unit. The question asks specifically for the meaningful unit `hesp`.
- The existing answer-key aside `kîtê/kitê` introduces an unnecessary spelling/form question not needed to justify the answer.
- **Proposed rationale-only replacement:** `Di §15 de, bêje yekeya zimanî ye ku wate û fikir digihîne. “Hesp” navê heywanekê nîşan dide; ji ber vê yekê di vê pirsê de bêje ye.`
- This removes the uncertain aside rather than adjudicating its orthography. No key change.

### L6 Q3 — noun modifier in `hespê beza`

- **Key A (`beza`) remains correct.**
- Source §59 (p. 61) explicitly treats `hespê beza` as an example of `bireserê navdêrê`, the element associated with and specifying the noun.
- The current stem shortens the term to bare `bireser`, despite the course's own warning that the noun-modifier term differs from clause-object `bireser`.
- **Proposed stem:** `Di koma navdêrî “hespê beza” de, bireserê navdêrê kîjan e?`
- Key unchanged.

### L6 Q4 — position of the noun modifier

- **Key B (`pêşî an paşî navdêrê`) remains correct.**
- Source §59 says noun complements/modifiers can be placed before or after the noun and gives examples of both.
- The bare current stem `Bireser dikare li ku were?` is unnecessarily ambiguous across Lessons 6 and 10.
- **Proposed stem:** `Bireserê navdêrê dikare li ku were?`
- Key unchanged.

### L8 Q3 — plural indefinite form in §66

- **Key A (`-in`) remains correct.**
- Source §66 (pp. 63–64) explicitly labels `EK, EK, IN` for the standalone/no-complement pattern and gives `hespin hatin` and `mehînin hatin`. §67 separately gives `-ekî/-eke/-ine` when a noun complement follows.
- The current phrase `bê bireser` is source-related, but risks being read as the clause-object term from Lesson 10.
- **Proposed stem:** `Di rewşa §66 de, dema piştî navdêrê hêmaneke raveker neyê, forma gelemjimara nebinavkirî çi ye?`
- This tests the §66 condition directly and preserves key A.

### L10 Q1 — paradigm membership vs syntactic object

- **Key B (`min`) remains correct as membership in the named paradigm.**
- Source §86 (p. 76) explicitly names the list `min, te, wî, wê, me, we, wan` as `Pronavên bireser`, while also explaining that in transitive past constructions these forms can change roles: `min tu dîtî`, `te ez dîtim`, etc.
- The source therefore supports the paradigm label but also confirms why a stem that sounds like a live clause-function question can mislead.
- **Proposed stem:** `Kîjan ji van forman di tabloya “Pronavên bireser” ya Ders 10 de hatiye rêzkirin?`
- Key unchanged.

### L10 Q2 — feminine form in the named paradigm

- **Key C (`wê`) remains correct under the course's paradigm table.**
- Source §86 supports the category and its construction-dependent behavior; the course table supplies the correspondence being tested.
- The current phrase `forma bireserî` can be read as an actual clause-object form/function rather than a table form.
- **Proposed stem:** `Di tabloya “Pronavên bireser” ya Ders 10 de, forma mê ya “ew” çi ye?`
- Key unchanged.

### L12 Q1 — adjective class scope

- **Key B remains correct for the descriptive-adjective subtype, not as an exhaustive definition of every adjective class.**
- Source §119 (p. 89) gives a broad definition of adjectives; §122 lists six types; §124 (p. 90) specifically says descriptive adjectives describe the noun's quality/attribute.
- The current broad stem `Rengdêr çi dike?` is wider than option B, which expresses the §124 descriptive-adjective function.
- **Proposed stem:** `Rengdêra wesfîn çi dike?`
- Key B unchanged.

### L12 Q3 — numeral adjective category label

- **Key A remains correct conceptually.**
- Source §§134–135 (p. 94) identifies numeral adjectives as a class that precedes nouns and specifies their number/quantity. The course's own §4 gives `sê mal` as an example.
- The semantic category is settled by the source; the remaining concern is the option's singular compound form `rengdêra jmarîn`, where the lesson consistently names the class `rengdêrên jmarîn`.
- **Proposed stem and options (avoid the disputed singular label):**
  `Di hevoka “sê mal” de, “sê” endamê kîjan koma rengdêran e?`
  - A) `rengdêrên jmarîn`
  - B) `rengdêrên wesfîn`
  - C) `daçek`
- Key A unchanged. This uses the course/source class label rather than guessing a singular inflection.
