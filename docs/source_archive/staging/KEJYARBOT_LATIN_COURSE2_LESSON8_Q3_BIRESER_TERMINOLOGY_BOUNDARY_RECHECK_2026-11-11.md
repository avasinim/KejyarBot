## Exact item

Lesson 8, Q3:

> Forma gelemjimar a nebinavkirî ya bê bireser çi ye? A) -in B) -ên C) -a

Key: **A — `-in`**.

Current consolidated rationale:

> Di paradigmaya §66 de `-in` forma gelemjimara nebinavkirî ye dema ku pêvekeke raveker li pey navdêrê tune be. Ev form ji `-ên`-a binavkirî û ji formên tewandî cuda ye.

## Exact lesson distinction

The reviewed lesson labels §66:

> Piştî navdêrê, bê pêvekeke raveker li pey navdêrê

and gives:
- `-ek` for masculine singular: `hespek hat`
- `-ek` for feminine singular: `mehînek hat`
- `-in` for plural: `hespin hatin`, `mehînin hatin`

It then separately labels §67:

> Piştî navdêrê, bi pêvekeke raveker li pey navdêrê

with forms `-ekî/-eke/-ine`.

The lesson's own summary explicitly distinguishes the paradigms `-ek/-ek/-in`, `-ekî/-eke/-ine` and `-î/-e/-en`. It does not describe §66 as “without an object” (bê bireser); it describes the absence of a following descriptive/qualifying element after the noun.

## Finding

**A real terminology mismatch is present in the quiz stem.** In this grammatical course, `bireser` is already used for a noun complement/qualifier in the term `bireserê navdêrê` (Lesson 6), and separately for the object of a clause (Lessons 9–10). The Lesson 8 §66 condition is not “without an object”; it is “without a descriptive element following the noun.”

The current rationale correctly states the source condition and therefore is more precise than the quiz stem. The key A remains source-supported. This is a stem-terminology issue, not evidence that the answer key is wrong.

## Suggested stem wording for editorial review

Preferred source-mirroring alternative:

> Forma gelemjimara nebinavkirî di rewşa bê hêmaneke raveker li pey navdêrê de çi ye? A) `-in` B) `-ên` C) `-a`

A shorter alternative, if the course editor confirms the phrase is idiomatic:

> Dema piştî navdêrê hêmaneke raveker neyê, forma gelemjimara nebinavkirî çi ye?

Keep the answer choices and key unchanged unless a separate review finds a substantive issue.

## Cross-lesson terminology impact

- Preserve `bireserê navdêrê` for the Lesson 6 noun-complement function.
- Preserve `bireser` for clause object in Lessons 9–10.
- For Lesson 8 §66, use `hêmaneke raveker li pey navdêrê` (or an editor-approved equivalent), not bare `bireser`.
- Do not perform global find/replace: the terms have distinct, context-dependent uses.
