## Cross-lesson consistency findings
1. Object NP vs clitic: «کتێبەکەی» in past-transitive examples must be segmented and functionally analyzed without labeling the hosted ـی as possessive by default. Align Lessons 12, 18, 25, 39, 40 and 42.
2. Complement vs adjunct: preserve the distinction between a selected complement and an optional adjunct; omission alone is not a universal diagnostic. Align Lessons 36 and 39.
3. Clause terminology: «پێڕستە»، «سەرڕستە»، «بەرڕستە/ژێرەڕستە»، «هاولکیی هاوئاست» and «هاولکیی بەرخەر» need one approved register across Lessons 37–38 and 46.
4. Clause count, not verb count: retain the clause-based sentence taxonomy in Lessons 37–38 and 46.
5. Recipient vs indirect object: preserve the provisional descriptive label until the owner approves a framework-wide choice. Align Lessons 39 and 41.
6. Transitivity vs lexical compound status vs voice: keep these as independent analytical dimensions. Align Lessons 40–44.
