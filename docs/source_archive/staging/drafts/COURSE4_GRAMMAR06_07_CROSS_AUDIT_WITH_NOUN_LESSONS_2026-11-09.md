## Lesson 6 findings: adjective definition and diagnostics
1. Correctly distinguishes adjective from adverb by what it modifies and by syntactic function. Preserve the contrast: «ئەسپەکە خێرایە» vs «ئەسپەکە خێرا ڕۆیشت».
2. Correctly warns that ezafe «ـی» alone does not determine word class. It may occur in noun–adjective and noun–noun relations; this aligns with Lesson 3's requirement not to treat every linker as an interfix.
3. The examples «منداڵی زیرەک» and «دیواری خانوو» are pedagogically useful because the first contains a noun modified by an adjective, while the second is a noun–noun relation. Keep this contrast explicit.
4. Open/closed adjectival ezafe is a more specialized syntactic/morphosyntactic topic. Keep the cited grammar/variety scope attached; do not generalize the exact surface patterns to all Sorani varieties without checking.
5. Potential wording refinement: distinguish “adjective as a lexical category” from “predicative adjective as a clause constituent/function”; avoid treating predicative position as a separate word class.

## Lesson 7 findings: adjective classification and morphology
1. Qualitative vs relational adjectives is a semantic classification; simple/derived/compound is a morphological/word-formation classification. These are independent axes, not competing subtypes.
2. The warning that not every word ending in «ـی» is relational adjective is important and should remain.
3. Demonstratives and interrogatives used with nouns may be analyzed as determiners in contemporary frameworks; independent forms such as «ئەمە» / «ئەوە» can be pronouns. Preserve the framework qualification rather than presenting one taxonomy as universal.
4. Cardinal/ordinal numerals are distinct from grammatical number (singular/plural). Preserve this distinction.
5. «بەهێز = بە + هێز» has dictionary support according to the lesson's cited unified dictionary. Retain source attribution and verify exact entry/page in final lesson references.
6. «ڕووخۆش» is attested as a lexical item meaning «خۆشڕوو» in هەنبانە بۆرینە; the internal analysis «ڕوو + خۆش» is pedagogical/structural interpretation and should not be described as an independently proven productive rule.
7. Prefix «بە-» and «بێ-» require lexeme-by-lexeme evidence for productivity and dialect scope. Do not infer that every theoretically possible combination is an established adjective.

## Cross-lesson terminology controls
- Keep noun vs adjective as separate lexical categories; syntactic function is determined in context.
- Keep ezafe/linker «ـی» separate from derivational suffix «ـی» and from any proposed interfix. The same written sign may have different grammatical analyses.
- Keep derivation (word formation) separate from inflection (number/definiteness).
- Keep relational adjective suffixation separate from abstract noun derivation and nominal genitive/ezafe.
- Keep cardinal/ordinal numeral category separate from singular/plural grammatical number.
- Use source/dialect labels whenever evidence is from a particular variety or dictionary tradition.
