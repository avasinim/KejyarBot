## Intended division of teaching responsibility

### Lesson 3 — Noun: definition and general properties
Keep the foundational definition, identification diagnostics, word class vs syntactic function, broad classification dimensions, and a concise introduction to simple/derived/compound structure. Its advanced interfix discussion should remain explicitly advanced and evidence-qualified. Lesson 3 should not become the full treatment of every noun subtopic.

### Lesson 4 — Noun: grammatical number and gender
Own the detailed explanation of singular/plural, plural markers, definite plural forms where relevant to number morphology, allomorphy/phonological transitions, and natural vs grammatical gender. Lesson 3 may preview these categories but should direct learners here for the full paradigm.
Important distinction: plural morphology (-ان, -ەکان and related forms) is inflection, not derivational word formation.

### Lesson 5 — Noun: semantic, definiteness and structural classifications
Own the detailed treatment of concrete/abstract, definiteness/indefiniteness, and the structural distinction simple/derived/compound. Lesson 3 may preview these as classification dimensions but should not duplicate the complete Lesson 5 exposition.
Keep referential classification (common/proper), number, definiteness, semantic type and morphological structure as independent axes.

## Confirmed overlap and editorial risks

1. **Definiteness is repeated across Lessons 3 and 5.** Lesson 3 currently gives the three-state overview and several suffix details; Lesson 5 gives a second, fuller account. Recommendation: Lesson 3 keeps a compact diagnostic overview and explicit cross-reference; Lesson 5 owns the complete explanation and paradigms.
2. **Number is repeated across Lessons 3 and 4, and partly Lesson 5.** Lesson 3 has extensive points on -ان, -ەکان, -گەل/-گال and collective nouns; Lesson 4 owns the full number system. Lesson 5 only needs definiteness examples that happen to show singular/plural. Avoid restating plural rules in Lesson 5.
3. **Natural vs grammatical gender is repeated across Lessons 3 and 4.** Lesson 3 should state the conceptual distinction briefly; Lesson 4 owns the detailed Sorani-specific conclusion and examples.
4. **Simple/derived/compound structure appears in Lessons 3 and 5.** Lesson 3 should introduce the three labels and one carefully chosen example each; Lesson 5 should own diagnostic tests, derivation/compounding examples, borrowing cautions and evidence.
5. **Noun as word class vs syntactic function is a Lesson 3 foundation.** Preserve it there; do not turn subject/object into noun subclasses in Lessons 4 or 5.
6. **Collective noun is not grammatical plural.** Lesson 3's semantic definition and Lesson 4's number system must cross-reference each other without equating collective semantics and plural inflection.
7. **Definiteness and proper-name reference are not identical.** Lesson 3 and Lesson 5 both state this correctly; preserve the distinction and avoid simplifying proper nouns as automatically bearing a morphological definite suffix.
8. **Morphological word formation vs inflection.** Keep derivation and compounding under Lesson 5 structural analysis; keep number/definiteness markers under inflectional morphology. Avoid describing -ان or -ەکان as derivational affixes.
9. **Dialect/source scope.** Claims based on Central Kurdish/Silemani studies must retain their variety label; do not generalize to all Sorani without independent evidence.

- Lesson 5: own the full semantic/definiteness/structural classification; add a concise cross-reference to Lesson 4 wherever plural morphology is mentioned.
- Add a shared terminology index only after the three lesson texts have been reconciled; do not silently normalize or replace existing Kurdish terms.
- Do not remove repeated content during the audit. Mark proposed consolidation as an editorial action requiring explicit review.
