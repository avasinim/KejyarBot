## Lesson 8 — adjective degrees
1. Keep positive, comparative and superlative as semantic/grammatical degrees: «جوان»، «جوانتر»، «جوانترین».
2. Distinguish morphological form from interpretation: «لە هەمووان جوانترە» is formally comparative with a superlative-like interpretation over a set.
3. Keep «هەرە جوان» separate from suffixal «جوانترین» unless corpus evidence establishes the exact construction and dialect scope.
4. Not every adjective naturally admits degree; absolute/classifying meanings can constrain comparison.
5. «خێرا» remains context-sensitive: adjective in «ئەسپەکە خێرایە» and adverb in «ئەسپەکە خێرا ڕۆیشت». This aligns with Lessons 6–7.

## Lesson 9 — numeral structure
1. The lesson correctly separates Numeral (lexical word class) from Grammatical Number (singular/plural inflection of nouns). Keep this distinction explicit and cross-reference Lesson 4.
2. Simple/derived/compound is a morphological analysis, not a classification by numerical size.
3. «دوو منداڵ» and «سەد پێنووس» are noun phrases, not compound words: numeral and noun remain separate words.
4. «دووشەممە، سێشەممە...» function as day names/nouns in context, not as numeral words in the sentence.
5. «یەکڕەنگ، یەکدڵ، دوودڵ، دووزمان» can be adjectives; «یەکبوون، یەکگرتن، یەکخستن» are verbal/infinitival lexemes. Their numeral element does not determine the final word class.
6. The listed suffix inventory (-انە، -مینە، -ێتی، -ینە، -ی، -ە، -ەک، -ۆک، -م، -ەوانە، -یی، -وو، -ین، -ەم، -ەمین) is not one uniform productive numeral suffix set. Analyze each form lexeme-by-lexeme, with source and context.
7. Regional/lexical examples such as «دووچوار، دووشەش، سێچوار، سێشەش» must remain candidates pending contextual corpus/dictionary evidence, not be taught as general productive patterns.
8. The rule “derived numerals do not take prefixes” is too categorical without broader evidence; phrase as an observation about the examples currently documented.

## Lesson 10 — independent personal pronouns
1. Preserve the six standard Sorani personal forms: من، تۆ، ئەو، ئێمە، ئێوە، ئەوان.
2. «ئێوە» is grammatically second-person plural but can be used honorifically for one addressee; distinguish grammatical number from pragmatic reference.
3. Distinguish the independent pronoun from person/number marking on the verb. In «ئێمە دەچین», the pronoun and verbal agreement marker are separate elements.
4. Subject function is not a word class. Pronouns can function as subjects, but subject/object are syntactic functions.
5. Keep Lesson 10's scope limited to independent personal pronouns; demonstratives, interrogatives and bound forms belong to their own lessons.
6. Verb agreement depends on tense/aspect and transitivity; avoid generalizing one present-tense paradigm to all past transitive clauses.

## Cross-lesson terminology controls
- Lesson 6/8: adjective vs adverb depends on syntactic use, not surface form alone.
- Lesson 7/9: numeral as word class is distinct from noun grammatical number.
- Lesson 3/10: noun and pronoun are lexical categories; subject/object are syntactic functions.
- Lesson 4/9: plural suffixes belong to inflectional number morphology, not numeral derivation.
- Lesson 5/9: compound lexeme vs phrase boundary must be established structurally; adjacency or shared numeral element is insufficient.
