## Main editorial finding
Jambrović & Hassan (2023), §§3–4, pp. 5–12, show why the same visible `-a` shape must not automatically be classified as an interfix: in Silemānī it can appear as a phrase-level linker in definite noun phrases, while compound-internal linkers are supported by additional diagnostics (single phonological word/final stress, specialized or non-compositional meaning, fixed internal order, and no insertion of the indefinite suffix inside the compound).
