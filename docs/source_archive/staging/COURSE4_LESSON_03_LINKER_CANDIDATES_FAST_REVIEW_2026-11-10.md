## One important distinction from Jambrović & Hassan (2023)
The article distinguishes two things that can look alike on the page:
- In Silemānī noun phrases, the linker -a/-ī can connect a head noun to a modifier. This is phrase-level linking/īzāfa, not automatically an interfix inside a lexical compound (pp. 5–8).
- In lexical compounds, -a can occur inside a single phonological word. Useful diagnostics include final stress on the whole compound, non-compositional or specialized meaning, fixed internal order, and the inability to insert the indefinite suffix between the internal elements (pp. 10–12).

The article's clear compound examples include:
- `rash-a-bā` «ڕەشەبا»
- `kar-a-kewī` «گاکێوی»
- `hawr-a-trīshqa` «هەورەتریشقە»
- `guł-a-bākh` «گوڵەباخ»

This is specific evidence for these forms in the Silemānī variety; it is not a blanket validation of every -ە- item in the owner's long list.

Use the broad learner-friendly description “جووتەوشە/پێکهاتەی هاوپەیوەند” where appropriate. Reserve the technical label “ناوبەندی وشەسازی” for items that meet the word-formation diagnostics.

### Best examples for the current lesson's short explanation
The 2024 study's reported examples already recorded in the previous audit are suitable as examples of paired/coordinate linking:
- `ئاو و هەوا`
- `دەغڵ و دان`
- `سەر و پێ`
- `خێر و بێر`
- `پڕ و پوچ`
- `سارد و گەرم`

For classroom simplicity, present these first as coordinated pairs. Then explain that some such pairs become lexicalized compounds, while ordinary `و` between two freely coordinated words is just a conjunction. The 2025 corpus study's `دەم‌وچاو` and `ڕەنگاوڕەنگ` can be shown as examples reported in its broader compounding inventory, with the caveat that the exact morphological analysis of each form is not fully demonstrated there.
