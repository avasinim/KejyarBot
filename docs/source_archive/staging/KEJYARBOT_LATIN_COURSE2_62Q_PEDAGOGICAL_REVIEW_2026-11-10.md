## Specific editorial cautions retained
1. **Reciprocal pronouns (Lesson 10, Q5):** keep the narrower “two or three people” wording because the reviewed lesson attributes that scope to the cited grammar section. A generalized “two or more” answer would require fresh source verification.
2. **Lesson 5 tense restriction:** preserve the explicit “dema niha” qualification in Q2–Q3 and the exact sentence in Q4; removing these would reintroduce ambiguity between present transitive object marking and past transitive agent marking.
3. **Lesson 6 terminology:** preserve the compound term `bireserê navdêrê`; do not shorten it to `bireser` where the latter may be read as a sentence function.
4. **Lesson 8 suffix systems:** do not merge `-ine` with definite `-ên`; they belong to different paradigms in this lesson.
5. **Lesson 11 `ko`:** preserve the contextual distinction between relative pronoun and conjunction.
