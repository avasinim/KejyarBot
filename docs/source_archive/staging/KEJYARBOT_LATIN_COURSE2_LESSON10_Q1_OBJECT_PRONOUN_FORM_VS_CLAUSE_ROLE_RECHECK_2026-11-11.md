Lesson 10 Q1:

> `min` di tabloya formên pronavên bireser de heye. Lê erkê wê di hevokê de divê ji avahiya hevokê were nasîn: di hin hevokên rabirdûya gerguhêz de `min` dikare kirarê tewandî be. Ji ber vê yekê forma pronavê û erkê wê di hevokê de yek tişt nînin.

## Exact lesson context

Lesson 10 labels the section and paradigm “Pronavên bireser” and lists `min, te, wî, wê, me, we, wan`. It then explicitly warns that these forms cannot be treated globally as subject or object: their clause role depends on construction. Its examples include:
- Present: `Ez te dibînim` — `te` is the object.
- Past: `Te ez dîtim` — `te` is the inflected agent and `ez` is the object.
- `Wê ew dît` — `wê` is the inflected agent and `ew` is the direct object.

Lesson 9 likewise describes `min/te/wî/wê/me/we/wan` as forms in a “forma kirarî” paradigm and cautions that their clause function is construction-dependent.

## Finding

The keyed answer is source-supported **if the stem is asking for membership in the course's paradigm labelled “pronavên bireser”**: `min` is in that listed set.

However, the stem “Kîjan yek ji pronavên bireser e?” can naturally be read as asking which pronoun is functioning as the grammatical object. Under that reading, the label is misleading: the lesson itself demonstrates `min` as an inflected agent in transitive past constructions and `ez` as the object in `Te ez dîtim`.

This is not a demonstrated key-letter error. It is a **form-class label versus syntactic-function ambiguity** in the stem, and it repeats the very distinction the rationale tries to teach.

## Suggested stem alternatives for editorial review

Preferred, if the course keeps its existing paradigm name:

> Kîjan ji van forman di tabloya “pronavên bireser” ya vê dersê de heye?

Alternative, more explicit about paradigm membership:

> Kîjan form di koma formên pronavên bireser de tê hejmartin?

The keyed answer remains B (`min`) under either proposed wording. Do not silently relabel the paradigm or alter the key without source/editorial adjudication.
