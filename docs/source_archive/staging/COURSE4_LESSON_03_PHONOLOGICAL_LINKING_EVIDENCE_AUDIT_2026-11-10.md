## 2. Critical phonetic-symbol distinction: Kurdish letter ج versus glide /j/
The source's “ناوبەندی (جـ)” examples are written with the Kurdish letter `ج` (the affricate /d͡ʒ/), e.g. `میوەجات`, `سەوزیجات`, `سەوزەجات`.

The phonological literature on glide insertion uses IPA `/j/` for the palatal approximant (the sound commonly represented by Kurdish `ی` in this environment), not the affricate /d͡ʒ/ represented by `ج`. These are different segments and must never be conflated:
- `ج` = /d͡ʒ/ (affricate).
- `ی` as a glide in the relevant phonological description = /j/ (palatal approximant).
- `و` may represent /w/ in glide environments, depending on phonological context and dialect.

Hasan & Rasheed (2016) analyze glide insertion in word-medial vowel sequences across Kurdish dialects. They report that /j/ is generally the default hiatus-resolution strategy, while /w/ insertion and vowel deletion occur in restricted dialectal and segmental environments. Their study uses stem-plus-suffix sequences produced by speakers from four Kurdish-speaking areas. It does **not** validate an inserted affricate /d͡ʒ/ in the specific forms `میوەجات` or `سەوزەجات`.

## 3. Assessment of the source's proposed `ج` between `میوە/سەوزی/سەوزە` and `-ات`
Owner-supplied analysis:
- `میوە + ج + ات = میوەجات`
- `سەوزی + ج + ات = سەوزیجات`
- `سەوزە + ج + ات = سەوزەجات`

Evidence status:
- The cited Kurdish glide-insertion study concerns /j/ and /w/ glides, not /d͡ʒ/; it cannot be used to support the proposed `ج`.
- The source itself describes `-ات` as an Arabic plural ending also used in Kurdish. That raises a separate morphological question: whether the forms are loan-pattern plurals, lexicalized forms, or productive Kurdish suffixation. This requires lexical and dialect evidence.
- No reliable source retrieved in this audit establishes a productive `ج` insertion rule in these three examples.

Provisional classification: **unverified lexical/morphological forms; not an established /d͡ʒ/ epenthesis rule**. Preserve the examples in a pending-evidence appendix only. Do not teach the equation `ج = hiatus-repair glide`.

## 4. Assessment of proposed `ه` in plural forms and ordinals
Owner-supplied examples:
- `دێ + ه + ات = دێهات`
- `میوە + ه + ات = میوەهات`
- `نۆ + ه + ەم/ەمین = نۆهەم/نۆهەمین`
- `دوو + ه + ەم/ەمین = دووهەم/دووهەمین`
- `سێ + ه + ەم/ەمین = سێهەم/سێهەمین`
- `دێهاتەکان`

These should be separated into at least two morphological questions:

### 4a. Ordinal suffix
Thackston's *Sorani Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*, §12.1, describes ordinal numerals as cardinal numerals plus the suffix `هەم`, with `ه` dropped after consonants. It lists `دووهەم، سێهەم، نۆهەم` among the ordinal forms. Thus the `ه` in `نۆهەم/دووهەم/سێهەم` is best taught provisionally as part of the ordinal suffix `-هەم` (with morphophonological realization conditioned by the stem ending), not as a free-standing interfix inserted between two independent words.
- Source: Thackston, W. M., *Sorani Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*, §12.1, ordinal numbers. Accessible transcription: https://kurdishsorani.blogspot.com/2014/05/grammar-substantives.html
- This source directly supports the ordinal suffix analysis; it does not establish an `ه` interfix in `دێهات` or `میوەهات`.

### 4b. Proposed `-ات` plural forms
The forms `دێهات` and `میوەهات` require separate lexical and morphological verification. The presence of `ه` before `-ات` cannot be assumed to be the same process as the ordinal suffix `-هەم`. Do not generalize from ordinals to plural forms.
- The form `دێهاتەکان` should be parsed only after the base plural form `دێهات` is independently validated.
- No universal `ه` insertion rule is adopted.

Provisional classification: **ordinal suffix -هەم: supported; proposed -ه- before -ات: unresolved**.

## 5. Assessment of proposed `د`
The owner-supplied source cuts off before explaining the `د` section. A separate phonological overview by Khasrow F. Almasi (2010), “Phonological Adjustments in Kurdish,” discusses insertion in Sorani verbal forms and gives examples such as `eçim` / `deçim`, describing /d/ at the beginning of present-tense verb forms. It also discusses deletion of /d/ in some noun forms and dialects. This is not evidence for a general consonant linker between two bases or for a `د` interfix in compounds.
- Source: https://kurdishacademy.org/?page_id=883 (sections “Insertion” and “Deletion”).
- Because the owner's actual section is truncated, this literature is only a related phonological reference, not a reconstruction of the missing owner text.

Provisional classification: **do not classify `د` as a compound interfix; possible relation to verbal present morphology/phonological insertion must be examined separately once the original section is recovered**.

## 6. Distinguish the categories in the teaching design
| Category | Description | Safe examples / status |
|---|---|---|
| Compound linker/interfix | Bound element in lexical word formation | `-ە-` and `-و-` only where the cited morphology study directly supports the specific structure |
| Glide insertion / hiatus repair | /j/ or /w/ inserted at vowel boundaries in phonological environments | Supported as a Kurdish phonological process; dialect and segmental context matter |
| Ordinal suffix | `-هەم`, with `ه` deletion after consonants | `دووهەم، سێهەم، نۆهەم` |
| Proposed `ج` before `-ات` | Claimed affricate insertion | Unverified; do not equate with glide /j/ |
| Proposed `ه` before `-ات` | Claimed plural-related linker | Unresolved; separate from ordinal suffix |
| Proposed `د` | Missing owner section; related literature discusses verbal morphology and insertion | Do not infer a compound-linker rule |
| `ر`, `ت` sections | Not present in retrieved source copies | Await exact source; no rule inferred |

## 7. Safe instructional recommendation
- Rename the second subsection from “ناوبەندی دەنگی” to a more precise working heading such as **“پرۆسە فۆنۆلۆژییەکانی پەیوەستکردن و چارەسەری بۆشایی بزوێنەکان”** (phonological linking and hiatus-resolution processes), pending final terminology review.
- Teach /j/ and /w/ glide insertion separately from morphological interfixes.
- Teach `-هەم` as an ordinal suffix, not an interfix.
- Keep `میوەجات، سەوزیجات، سەوزەجات، دێهات، میوەهات` as unresolved lexical/morphological items pending dictionary/corpus and dialect evidence.
- Keep `د` unresolved until its missing source text is recovered.
- Do not add `ر` or `ت` without the exact owner source.
- Any future activity should ask learners to distinguish: a bound morpheme, an affix, a glide inserted to resolve hiatus, a free word, and a lexicalized form. Do not ask learners to memorize the unverified `ج/ه/د` labels.
