## Purpose

This blueprint responds to the learning-outcome/quiz-alignment audit. It supplies candidate supplementary items for content currently taught but not directly sampled by the five-question quizzes. These are not approved replacements. Keep the original five questions in each lesson unchanged until the course owner approves a later revision.

Each candidate includes one intended answer and a rationale. Before adoption, compare every item with the printed source and the exact lesson wording; where the lesson gives a source-specific classification, retain that attribution.

## A. Course 1 — cumulative review bank (Lesson 12)

### C1-12-A: I / Î recognition
**Question:** Kîjan cot herfa I û Î bi awayê rast nîşan dide?
- A) jin / pîr
- B) jin / jin
- C) pîr / pîr
- D) jin / jîn

**Intended answer:** A) jin / pîr

**Rationale:** The first word contains I and the second contains Î. This samples the focused contrast from Lessons 6 and 12. It checks letter recognition in words, not a claim that the words are a minimal pair.

### C1-12-B: U / Û recognition
**Question:** Di kîjan cotê de peyva yekem U û ya duyem Û dihewîne?
- A) kur / dûr
- B) bûn / gur
- C) kûr / kul
- D) mû / kum

**Intended answer:** A) kur / dûr

**Rationale:** This checks both categories in a single comparison. Do not describe the two words as a minimal pair; they are examples containing the target letters.

### C1-12-C: V / W source contrast
**Question:** Kîjan cot di çavkaniya kursê de ji bo nîşandana cudahiya V û W hatiye bikaranîn?
- A) dev / dew
- B) dev / dev
- C) vir / vir
- D) ava / ava

**Intended answer:** A) dev / dew

**Rationale:** Reuses the source-attested contrast pair and checks the exact distinction taught in Lesson 8.

### C1-12-D: source classification of Y
**Question:** Li gorî dabeşkirina Celadet di vê kursê de, Y di kîjan komê de tê hesibandin?
- A) Di koma 23 herfên dengdar de
- B) Di koma 8 dengdêran de
- C) Wek nîşana dirêjiyê
- D) Wek herfeke bê formê biçûk

**Intended answer:** A) Di koma 23 herfên dengdar de

**Rationale:** Explicitly attributes the classification to the course source, avoiding an overgeneralized modern phonetic claim.

## B. Course 2 — Lesson 10 supplementary bank

### C2-10-A: compound pronoun formation
**Question:** Pronavê lihevxistî `jê` ji kîjan du hêmanan pêk hatiye?
- A) `ji` + forma pronavê
- B) `li` + forma pronavê
- C) `bi` + forma pronavê
- D) `di` + forma pronavê

**Intended answer:** A) `ji` + forma pronavê

**Rationale:** Tests a distinct subsection not sampled by the current five-item quiz. The question avoids choosing one gender/person expansion because the lesson lists several source equivalents.

### C2-10-B: reflexive `xwe`
**Question:** Di hevoka `Wê xwe kuşt` de `xwe` çi nîşan dide?
- A) Kesekî din
- B) Heman kesê ku kirar e
- C) Cihê kirinê
- D) Gelemjara kesan

**Intended answer:** B) Heman kesê ku kirar e

**Rationale:** Directly assesses the reflexive function taught in the `xwe` subsection.

### C2-10-C: neuter `ê`
**Question:** Li gorî vê dersê, `ê` wek pronavê nêtar bi gelemperî çi nîşan dide?
- A) Yekjimar
- B) Gelemjimar
- C) Tenê kesê yekem
- D) Tenê zayenda mê

**Intended answer:** A) Yekjimar

**Rationale:** Tests the explicit contrast in the lesson between neuter singular `ê` and plural `wan`.

## D. Course 2 — Lesson 12 rotating question bank

The lesson covers six adjective classes, degrees of comparison, and a separate `jmarnav` category. Five fixed questions cannot sample every branch reliably. The following are candidates for a rotating bank; do not add all of them to a single short quiz without reviewing its length.

### C2-12-A: demonstrative adjective
**Question:** Di `ev mirov` de `ev` çi erkêkî heye?
- A) Rengdêra îşarkî
- B) Rengdêra wesfîn
- C) Rengdêra jmarîn
- D) Rengdêra xwemalîn

**Intended answer:** A) Rengdêra îşarkî

### C2-12-B: interrogative adjective
**Question:** Di `kîjan mirov hat?` de `kîjan` çi ye?
- A) Rengdêra pirsiyarkî
- B) Rengdêra nebinavkirî
- C) Rengdêra wesfîn
- D) Rengdêra îşarkî

**Intended answer:** A) Rengdêra pirsiyarkî

### C2-12-C: indefinite adjective
**Question:** Di `gelek fêkî` de `gelek` çi ye?
- A) Rengdêra nebinavkirî
- B) Rengdêra wesfîn
- C) Rengdêra xwemalîn
- D) Rengdêra îşarkî

**Intended answer:** A) Rengdêra nebinavkirî

### C2-12-D: ordinal adjective
**Question:** Di `stûna çaran` de `çaran` çi nîşan dide?
- A) Rêza tiştê
- B) Rengê tiştê
- C) Xwedîtiya tiştê
- D) Nêzîkî tiştê

**Intended answer:** A) Rêza tiştê

**Rationale:** This samples the ordinal adjective material in the reviewed lesson. Keep the term `rêzîn` distinct from the separately classified ordinal `jmarnav` subsection.

### C2-12-E: comparison degree
**Question:** Di rêza `mezin — mezintir — herî mezin` de `mezintir` kîjan astê nîşan dide?
- A) Asta bingehîn
- B) Asta berawirdî
- C) Asta herî bilind
- D) Jmarnavê nebinavkirî

**Intended answer:** B) Asta berawirdî

**Rationale:** The lesson teaches degrees of descriptive adjectives, but none of the current five quiz items tests them.
