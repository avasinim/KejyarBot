## Finding and clarification
Lesson 9 presents `min/te/wî/wê/me/we/wan` in a column named `Forma kirarî`. A learner could mistake the label of the form for a claim that the pronoun is always the syntactic agent/subject. Lesson 10 already explains that the syntactic role depends on the transitive-past clause structure and distinguishes direct from indirect objects.

A short note was therefore added after the Lesson 9 examples:
- `forma kirarî` names the form used in the described construction;
- it does not mean the form is the agent in every sentence;
- identify the role from the verb and sentence structure, with a cross-reference to Lesson 10.
