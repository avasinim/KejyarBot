## 1. Question under review

Lesson 12 uses three forms around the label for numeral adjectives:

- `rengdêrên jmarîn`
- `rengdêrê jmarîn`
- `rengdêra jmarîn`

The reviewed lesson's quiz, Q3, currently keys option A as `rengdêra jmarîn` for `sê` in `sê mal`. The lesson body and category heading use different forms. The goal is to avoid normalizing a grammatical form based only on visual similarity.

## 3. Provisional editorial recommendation

1. Preserve `rengdêrên jmarîn` when referring to the category/class in the plural.
2. For singular prose referring to the category concept, provisionally prefer `rengdêrê jmarîn`, because this is the form used in the source-aligned lesson's explicit contrast with `jmarnav`.
3. Do **not** silently retain or globally replace `rengdêra jmarîn` in Q3 yet. Mark it as a likely within-lesson inconsistency and ask a Kurmanji language editor to determine whether it is a legitimate case/construct variant in the quiz's exact syntactic environment or a typo.
4. Do not change `jmarnav`; its conceptual distinction from a numeral adjective is consistently taught in the lesson.

This is a provisional editorial preference, not a definitive grammatical ruling. It is based on internal course-text consistency, not direct verification of the original grammar book's page image for Lesson 12.

## 4. What remains unverified

- The primary source page image for the Lesson 12 adjective/numeral passage was not re-opened in this pass.
- The precise grammatical environment of the bare answer label in Q3 must be considered by a Kurmanji editor before deciding between `rengdêrê`, `rengdêra`, or another inflected form.
- The course text's own use of `rengdêrên jmarîn` as a plural class label does not, by itself, prove the correct singular/construct morphology in every sentence.
