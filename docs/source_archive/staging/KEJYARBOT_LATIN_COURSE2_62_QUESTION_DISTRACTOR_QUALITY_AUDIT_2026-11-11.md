## 3. Highest-priority item

### L10 Q2 — “Forma bireserî ya `ew` mê çi ye?” (key C: `wê`)

**Finding: unresolved substantive ambiguity; do not certify as one-best-answer yet.**

- Lesson 10's own explanation distinguishes the form of the pronoun from its role in the clause and gives `Wê ew dît`: `wê` is explicitly the inflected agent/subject (`kirarê tewandî`), while `ew` is the direct object (`bireserê rasterast`).
- The question asks for the “`forma bireserî`” of feminine `ew`, but keys `wê`. If `bireserî` is read as “object form”, the lesson's own example points to `ew`, not `wê`. If the term is intended in a different technical sense (e.g. an oblique/inflected paradigm label), that sense is not made sufficiently explicit in the item.
- Options `wî/ew/wê` are parallel pronoun forms, but this does not repair the terminology mismatch.
- **Required decision:** a qualified Kurmanji editor must define what `forma bireserî` means in this paradigm and reconcile it with `forma kirarî` and the `Wê ew dît` example. Only then should the item and key be revised together. Do not silently change C to another option.
