## L12 Q1 — broad category question, narrow keyed option

Exact stem and options:

> Rengdêr çi dike?  
> A) Têkiliya navdêrê bi lêkerê re diyar dike  
> B) taybetmendî an taybetiya navdêrê rave dike  
> C) Kes û hejmarê lêkerê li gorî kiryarê diguherîne

Key: **B**.

The current Lesson 12 opening definition says an adjective is a grammatical word associated with a noun and indicates its property, demonstrative reference, number, interrogative value, indefiniteness or possession. It then explicitly divides adjectives into six groups:
- wesfîn
- îşarkî
- jmarîn
- pirsiyarkî
- nebinavkirî
- xwemalîn

The lesson separately says that **wesfîn** adjectives describe the noun's property (e.g. `hespê mezin`). Thus option B accurately describes the *wesfîn subtype* and the prototypical descriptive function, but does not fully describe the broader six-part category as taught in the same lesson. For example, the lesson's own numeral/interrogative/possessive categories do not all reduce to describing a qualitative property.

This is a scope mismatch between the broad stem (“What does an adjective do?”) and the narrower answer option, not evidence that key B is wholly wrong.

**Suggested stem for editorial review, preserving option B and key B:**

> Rengdêra wesfîn çi dike?

Alternative:

> Rengdêrên wesfîn çi diyar dikin?

If the intention is to test the whole adjective category, the answer option should instead be expanded to cover the six functions named in the lesson. Do not rewrite the option or key without editorial approval.

## L12 Q3 — category is supported; exact form of the label remains open

Exact stem and options:

> `sê` di `sê mal` de çi ye?  
> A) rengdêra jmarîn  
> B) rengdêra wesfîn  
> C) daçek

Key: **A**.

Lesson 12 §4 calls the class `Rengdêrên jmarîn`, explains that this class specifies the number of the noun, and gives `sê mal`, `çar xanî` and `deh bizin` as examples. The classification of `sê` as a numeral adjective is therefore supported by the lesson's own explicit explanation and example. The answer key's semantic/category choice A is supported.

The unresolved issue is only the inflected/construct form of the option label: `rengdêra jmarîn` versus the alternative raised in earlier review, `rengdêrê jmarîn`. The course prose uses the plural class label `rengdêrên jmarîn`; it does not, by itself, settle which singular form is idiomatic in this exact option context. No sufficiently authoritative native-Kurmanji morphology ruling or directly inspectable source passage was available in the fetched repository files. Do not choose between the two forms by analogy or guess.

**Disposition:** retain key A; keep the exact option wording on hold for a qualified native-Kurmanji editor. A temporary editorial alternative is to use the class label in a fuller construction, but no replacement is approved in this audit.
