## 1. L10 Q2 — `wê` and the label “forma bireserî”
**Finding: source-supported answer form; the apparent contradiction is a form/function distinction, not proof that the keyed option is wrong.**
- Source §86 lists `min, te, wî, wê, me, we, wan` as direct-object pronoun forms in the basic construction.
- The same source section explains that in transitive past constructions the roles can switch. Thus `wê` may appear as the inflected agent in `Wê ew dît`, while `ew` is the direct object in that sentence.
- The reviewed lesson already states that the label “forma kirarî” names the form in the paradigm and does not mean that the form is always the clause's subject. It separately explains role switching in Lesson 10.
- Consequently, the answer `wê` to “forma bireserî ya ew mê çi ye?” is supported by the source's paradigm, provided the question is understood as asking for the corresponding form in the object-pronoun inventory, not asserting that `wê` must be a direct object in every clause.
**Editorial recommendation:** do not change the answer letter solely because `wê` is agent in `Wê ew dît`. Improve the rationale to say explicitly: “Di tabloya formên bireserî ya §86 de forma mê ya `ew` `wê` ye; lê di hevoka rabirdûya gerguhêz de, wek `Wê ew dît`, `wê` kirarê tewandî ye û `ew` bireser e. Navê forma pronavê û erkê wê di hevokê de yek tişt nînin.” Consider adding “di tabloya formên bireserî de” to the stem only if an editor judges that necessary for learner clarity.
**Disposition:** downgrade from source-conflict hold to wording/clarity review. No key change proposed.

## 2. L12 Q1 — adjective definition scope
**Finding: the current opening definition is incomplete if read as an absolute definition.**
- Source §§119–123 characterizes adjectives by their relation to nouns and identifies six classes.
- Source §§124–127 explicitly allows descriptive adjectives to occur predicatively and without an expressed noun; substantivized adjective uses may show noun-like inflection/determiner behaviour.
- Therefore a short definition such as “a word that accompanies a noun and describes it” is a useful introductory prototype but is too narrow as a categorical rule.
**Editorial recommendation:** add a qualification to the lesson's definition and to the answer rationale: “Rengdêr bi gelemperî navdêrê rave dike an taybet dike; lê hin rengdêr dikarin di erkê pêşbirkirinê de an bê navdêrekî eşkere jî werin bikaranîn.” Keep the six-class taxonomy as the course's adopted framework. Before finalizing Q1, verify that the keyed option and distractors test the intended core definition rather than excluding predicative/substantivized uses.
**Disposition:** remains held pending an authorized lesson/quiz wording revision and a fresh key-option review. This audit does not alter the answer letter.
