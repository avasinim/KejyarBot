## Evidence decisions to carry forward
- Huseen & Shuani (2024) supports specific `-ە-` and `-و-` compound patterns only; it does not validate the entire owner-supplied candidate lists.
- Jambrović & Hassan (2023) concerns nominal linkers in the Silemani variety and must not be generalized beyond its scope.
- Hasan & Rasheed (2016) supports Kurdish glide insertion /j/ and context-/dialect-limited /w/ insertion or vowel deletion; this is phonological hiatus repair, not automatically morphological interfixation.
- Thackston §12.1 supports ordinal suffix `-هەم`; the `ه` in ordinal forms is not to be taught as a free compound interfix.
- The owner-supplied `ج` section uses the affricate letter `ج`; it must not be confused with IPA /j/ glide (typically represented by `ی`).
- The `د` source section is truncated in both retrieved owner files. The `ر` and `ت` sections were not found in those copies. Do not infer missing content.
