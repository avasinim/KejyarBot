## Core decision
In this course, the preferred technical term for linguistic *context* is **بەستێن**. The correct Sorani inflection depends on syntax: بەستێن، بەستێنی، بەستێندا، بەستێنەوە, etc. Do not blindly replace substrings; rewrite the phrase grammatically.

## Resolved semantic groups
1. **Possessive ambiguity (Lessons 11–12, 18):** use forms of **بەستێنی خاوەنداری** where the point is to distinguish possessive clitics from transitive-past subject marking. This does not mean context alone decides; person marking, transitivity, constituent structure and intended reading must also be checked.
2. **Tense/aspect readings (Lessons 13, 15–16):** use **بەستێنی کاتی**, **بەستێنی عادەتی**, **بەستێنی داهاتوو** as appropriate. Distinguish the linguistic context from a temporal adverb such as «بەیانی» or «هەموو ڕۆژێک». The adverb contributes evidence; it is not itself the whole context.
3. **Respect/politeness (Lesson 10):** use **بەستێنی ڕێز** for the social-pragmatic setting of plural pronoun used politely for singular addressee.
4. **Natural speech/corpus evidence (Lessons 3, 8, 27):** use **بەستێنی بەکارهێنان** or **بەستێنی قسەی سروشتی** where the claim depends on actual speakers/corpus evidence. Do not conflate this with a generic situation or assert corpus validation that has not been done.
5. **Definiteness and reference (Lessons 4–5):** use **بەستێنی ڕستە** / **بەستێن** for recoverability of reference, alongside shared knowledge and grammatical marking. Preserve the fact that these are independent sources of definiteness.
6. **Draft Lesson 32:** retain its draft status; the suggested terminology does not activate it.
