## 2. Terminology decision
The user's preferred equivalent for linguistic *context* is **بەستێن**. It should be used consistently where the intended meaning is the linguistic, grammatical, semantic or discourse context that helps interpret a form or construction.

However, a mechanical replacement is not approved. A few recurring constructions need contextual editing:
- `سیاقی عادەت`, `سیاقی داهاتوو`, `سیاقی ڕابردوو`: consider **بارودۆخی عادەت/داهاتوو/ڕابردوو** when the phrase denotes a temporal situation or use-condition, rather than the interpretive context itself. Where the sentence is explicitly about linguistic interpretation, **بەستێنی کاتی** may be retained.
- `سیاقی گونجاو`: **بەستێنی گونجاو** is a strong candidate when it means the sentence/discourse environment needed for an interpretation.
- `سیاقی خاوەنداری`: likely **بەستێنی خاوەنداری** when distinguishing a possessive reading from another grammatical analysis.
- `سیاقی قسەی سروشتی` / examples / corpus evidence: use **بەستێنی بەکارهێنان** or a more explicit phrase such as **بارودۆخی بەکارهێنان** according to whether the sentence means linguistic environment or real-life circumstances.
- If a phrase means surrounding textual material, do not force بەستێن; assess **دەوروبەری دەق/ڕستە**.
