## Editorial findings
- The mechanical map is not a publishable patch: suffix/ezafe/locative forms such as `بەستێنەوە`, `بەستێنەکەدا`, and combinations with `لە` require grammatical rewriting rather than token substitution.
- Prefer the construction that expresses the intended relation: `لە بەستێنێکدا`, `لە بەستێنی گونجاودا`, `بەپێی بەستێن`, or `بە بەستێنەوە` only where the complete sentence warrants it. These are alternatives, not global replacements.
- Where the intended concept is an external state or real-world situation, use **بارودۆخ** rather than force **بەستێن**.
- Where the source explicitly means adjacent text, use **دەوروبەری دەق/ڕستە**.
- Where the claim depends on natural speech, corpus evidence or speaker usage, rewrite as **بەکارهێنانی قسەکەر / داتای قسەی سروشتی** and do not imply that corpus verification has already happened.
- Keep dialect scope explicit, especially in Lessons 3–5 and 27.
- Quiz answer values, answer indices and option ordering are not to be changed by terminology edits.
