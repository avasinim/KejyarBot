## The lesson's explicit distinction

Lesson 6 is titled **Bireserê Navdêrê**. It defines this as a word or group of words that describes, specifies or adds information about a noun; examples include `hespê beza`, `mehîna spehî` and `xencera min`.

Crucially, the lesson itself has a heading **Cudahîya termînolojî** and states:
- `bireserê navdêrê` in Lesson 6 is a noun modifier/complement;
- it is not the same as clause `bireser`;
- in Lesson 10, `pronavên bireser` concerns pronouns taking the direct-object role in a clause;
- therefore the bare word `bireser` alone is insufficient; the construction and its role must be identified.

## Exact quiz items

**L6 Q3**

> Di `hespê beza` de bireser çi ye? A) beza B) hesp C) hespê

Key: **A — `beza`**.

In the phrase `hespê beza`, `beza` is the post-head noun modifier, i.e. the lesson's `bireserê navdêrê`. The answer is supported by the intended lesson context, but the stem abbreviates the technical term to bare `bireser`, precisely the ambiguous form the lesson warns against.

Suggested clarification:

> Di koma navdêrî `hespê beza` de, bireserê navdêrê kîjan e?

Key A unchanged.

**L6 Q4**

> Bireser dikare li ku were? A) tenê di dawiya qisetê B) pêşî an paşî navdêrê C) tenê pêşî lêkerê

Key: **B — pêşî an paşî navdêrê**.

The lesson's explanation and examples concern the position of `bireserê navdêrê` relative to its head noun. The bare stem `Bireser` can be read as asking about the clause object, which is a different relation in Lesson 10.

Suggested clarification:

> Bireserê navdêrê dikare li ku were?

Key B unchanged.

## Finding

These are not proven answer-key errors. The intended answers are supported by the Lesson 6 context. However, the two stems omit the full technical term even though Lesson 6 explicitly says that the bare term `bireser` is insufficient and distinguishes the term from the clause object taught in Lesson 10.

This is a concrete cross-lesson terminology consistency issue. It should be resolved by clarifying the stems, not by globally replacing or redefining `bireser`.

## Cross-lesson control

- Lesson 6: retain full term `bireserê navdêrê` whenever the noun-modifying relation is intended.
- Lesson 10: use `bireser` for clause object/function and keep paradigm label distinct from actual clause role.
- Lesson 8: the separate `bê bireser` phrase concerns a descriptive element after the noun; do not assume it means the same thing as either Lesson 6's noun modifier or Lesson 10's clause object. Its own clarification proposal remains under review.
- Do not change answer letters: L6 Q3 A and L6 Q4 B remain supported.
