**Date:** 2026-11-10

## Finding
The course uses the term `bireser` in two compounds with different grammatical scopes:
- `bireserê navdêrê` in Lesson 6: a word or phrase that describes/specifies a noun (noun modifier).
- `pronavên bireser` in Lesson 10: pronoun forms used in the direct-object role in the relevant clause structures.

Without an explicit note, learners could conflate a noun modifier with a clause-level object.

## Editorial changes
1. Added a terminology note in Lesson 6 distinguishing `bireserê navdêrê` from the clause-level `bireser` in Lesson 10. It instructs learners to interpret the full compound and grammatical function, not the isolated word.
2. In Lesson 8, replaced the ambiguous phrase `rengdêr an bireser` with `rengdêr an hêmaneke din a raveker` in the explanation of what may follow an indefinite noun form. The examples and suffix rules were preserved.
