### L1 Q5 — composition of a phrase

**Current key:** B — `bi peyhevketina koman`.

**Candidate options**
- A) bi peyhevketina tîpan tenê
- B) bi peyhevketina koman

Check that `koman` is consistently used for the relevant constituent level in the lesson before approval.

## Separate coverage proposal — requires answer-key letter change

### L4 Q5 — distinguish `hespan` from `hespin`

The existing Q5 asks only what `hespin` indicates, repeating the plural recognition already tested by L4 Q2. A future authorized revision could test the contrast the lesson explicitly teaches:

**Candidate stem:** `Cudahiya “hespan” û “hespin” çi ye?`

- A) Her du formên tewandî ne.
- B) `hespan` forma tewandî ye; `hespin` forma gelemjimara nebinavkirî ye.
- C) `hespan` forma binavkirî ye; `hespin` forma yekjimar e.

**Candidate key:** B (this changes the current Q5 key from C to B).

**Source basis:** The Lesson 4 text explicitly distinguishes `hespan`/`mehînan` as oblique forms from `hespin`/`mehînin` as indefinite plural forms. This candidate broadens coverage rather than repeating simple recognition. It must be checked against the course's exact intended terminology before any application.
