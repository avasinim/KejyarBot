## Lesson 7 — Veqetandekên Binavkirî
### Source-supported
- The draft's three definite forms `-ê`, `-a`, `-ên` correspond to §§61–64 (pp.62–63).
- The examples `hespê Soro`, `deriyê malê`, `mehîna Cemşîd`, `ava tenik`, `hespên Soro`, and `mehînên Cemşîd` correspond to the source's masculine singular, feminine singular, and plural patterns.
- The source also treats shortened/independent forms such as `ê`, `a`, `ên` before an adjective when the noun is understood from context. The draft's examples `Ez ê gewir dixwazim`, `Ez a sor dixwazim`, and `Ez ên sor dixwazim` are a teaching presentation of this construction; retain the explanation that the noun is omitted/understood, and do not conflate these with suffixes attached after a noun.

### Review notes
1. The draft correctly distinguishes definiteness marking from inflection. These are separate grammatical functions, even where forms occur together in a word.
2. The examples with `ê/a/ên` before adjectives should be taught as substantivized/elliptical forms with an understood noun, not as ordinary suffixes placed before an adjective.
3. The source's own terminology and examples should be retained distinctly from the draft's modern teaching labels. The explanatory labels are editorial, not direct quotations.
4. No substantive source contradiction was identified in the core rules or examples in these two lessons during this pass.
