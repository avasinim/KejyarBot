## Finding
Lesson 12 already distinguishes six adjective classes from the separately presented numeral-pronoun category. However, its opening definition could be read too broadly, and the contrast between a numeral adjective and a numeral pronoun needed a clearer functional test.

## Editorial clarification
1. The adjective definition now explicitly says that an adjective accompanies a noun and identifies/describes it. The six classes remain the same.
2. The numeral-adjective section is clarified as a form that accompanies a noun and indicates its number or order, e.g. `sê mal` and `stûna çaran`.
3. The numeral-pronoun section now explicitly frames `jmarnav` as a separate category **within this course's classification**, and tells learners to examine the relation to the noun and the word's function in the sentence rather than relying on form alone.
4. The exercise instruction was refined to reuse `sê mal` as a contrastive example.
