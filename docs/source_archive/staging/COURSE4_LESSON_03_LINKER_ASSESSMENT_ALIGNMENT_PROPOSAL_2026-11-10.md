## Current alignment
The current runtime contains one quiz item about the distinction between `-ە-` and `-و-`:
- Q8 asks which statement is more precise.
- Correct answer: option 1, describing `-ە-` as occurring in compound structures and `-و-` as often joining paired elements, while noting that every example requires examination.

This is a useful introductory check, but it does not yet assess whether learners can distinguish:
1. a bound linker/interfix inside a compound;
2. the free conjunction `و`;
3. a free preposition such as `لە` or `بە`;
4. a lexicalized or reduplicative paired expression;
5. a phonological linking consonant such as the proposed `ج` or `ه`.

The current three activities focus on noun classification and syntactic roles; none directly tests the word-formation/linker material. The five exercises include one broad question on simple/derived/compound nouns, but none asks learners to test boundness, phrase-vs-word status, or the difference between morphological and phonological linking. The comprehension list also has no direct question on the newly supplied linker series.

## Assessment risk
Adding the full unverified candidate lists to a quiz would prematurely teach them as settled facts. The assessment should first teach the analytical distinction and use only securely supported examples. Candidate forms such as `داخلەدڵ، کەسنەزان، سەرتاپا، تاوناتاو، شارەوشار، ڕەنگاوڕەنگ، بەرانبەر، جێبەجێ` should not be used as unquestioned answer keys until individually verified.

## Non-destructive proposed additions
The following are proposals only; do not append them to runtime until the source review and teaching layout are approved.

### Proposed activity — bound linker or free word?
Give learners two groups:
- Research-backed compound examples from Huseen & Shuani (2024), selected exactly from the paper's own examples for `-ە-` and `-و-`.
- Free phrases containing the conjunction `و` or preposition `لە/بە`.

Ask them to mark the boundaries, identify whether the middle element is bound or free, and justify the decision using syntactic independence, stress/phonological-word behavior, meaning, and source evidence. Do not treat spelling alone as proof.

### Proposed exercise — test the analysis
For each item, learners must choose one provisional description:
- bound compound linker supported by the cited source;
- free grammatical word in a phrase;
- reduplicative/paired lexical candidate;
- phonological linking consonant candidate;
- insufficient evidence.

They must explain what additional evidence is needed before a form can be called a productive interfix.

### Proposed comprehension question
“بۆچی هەر توخمێک کە لەنێوان دوو بەشی وشەدا دەردەکەوێت، بە خۆکار ناوبەندی مۆرفۆلۆژی نییە؟”
Expected answer: because it may instead be a free word, part of a reduplicative construction, a lexicalized sequence, or a phonological bridge; classification requires morphological, syntactic, phonological, semantic, dialect, and corpus evidence.
