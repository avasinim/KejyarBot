## The form `yazde`

The lesson's `yazde` is retained. It is a supplementary example, not one of the source's three Y examples. Its spelling is not treated as an error: Michael L. Chyet's *Kurdish-English Dictionary* records `yazde` as an attested form (with `yanzdeh` as a cross-referenced form), and Geoffrey Haig's description lists `yanzdeh, yazdeh` in standard orthography. The lesson does not claim that `yazde` is the sole or preferred modern standard spelling.
