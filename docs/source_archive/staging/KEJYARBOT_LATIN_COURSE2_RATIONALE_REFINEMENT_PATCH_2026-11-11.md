## 3. Newly source-adjudicated row — L10 Q2

**Key retained: C (wê).** The source adjudication of §86 establishes that wê is the feminine form in the course's direct-object pronoun paradigm. In a transitive past clause such as Wê ew dît, the same form functions as the inflected agent, while ew is the direct object. The paradigm label and clause role must therefore be distinguished.

**Proposed rationale:**

> Di tabloya formên bireserî ya §86 de forma mê ya ew wê ye. Lê di hevoka rabirdûya gerguhêz de, wek Wê ew dît, wê kirarê tewandî ye û ew bireser e. Navê forma pronavê û erkê wê di hevokê de yek tişt nînin.

This row is now eligible for editorial wording review; the previous claim of a demonstrated source contradiction is withdrawn. No answer-key letter change is proposed.

## Terminology correction — L6 Q5 possessive modifier (2026-11-11)

The L6 Q5 refinement in Section 2 incorrectly calls `min` in `xencera min` a `pronavê xwemalî`. Under the reviewed Lesson 12 taxonomy, `xencera min` parallels `hespê min` and is a possessive adjective (`rengdêra xwemalîn`) modifying an expressed noun. `Pronavên xwemalîn` are forms such as `yê min/ya min/yên min` that replace an understood noun/group. Use the corrected proposed wording in `docs/source_archive/staging/KEJYARBOT_LATIN_COURSE2_L6_Q5_POSSESSIVE_MODIFIER_RATIONALE_CORRECTION_2026-11-11.md`, SHA `f9ec5ed10da96c889393b9ab1502e648b937d380`. The key remains C; no answer-key or lesson text changed. This correction supersedes only the L6 Q5 sentence in the refinement table.
