## Controlled findings for curriculum planning

1. The noun chapter itself has at least two explicit classification axes: **structure** and **meaning**; further sub-classifications appear in attributed accounts and later authorial discussion.
2. The book deliberately preserves or reports several different grammarians' definitions and taxonomies. These are evidence of source variation, not permission to harmonize them silently.
3. English and Persian terminology appears as comparative/attributed material. Retain attribution and do not present those categories as the author's own Kurdish taxonomy.
4. Some labels on pp.17, 22–24 and examples in the tables remain provisional. They are not approved lesson vocabulary.
5. This review does not establish a lesson count, quiz content, or runtime readiness.
